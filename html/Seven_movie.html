SPOTSTAR - SEVEN

Se7en is a gripping psychological crime thriller that follows two detectives, Somerset and Mills, as they hunt a serial killer whose murders are based on the seven deadly sins. Somerset, a seasoned and methodical investigator nearing retirement, is paired with Mills, a young and impulsive detective new to the city. Their pursuit begins with a gruesome murder representing gluttony, leading them into a series of disturbing crime scenes, each meticulously crafted to symbolize a sin. The killer, John Doe, leaves no clues beyond his elaborate, moral-driven message about humanity’s corruption. As the investigation deepens, the tension between Somerset’s caution and Mills’s recklessness grows. Each crime scene becomes more horrific, pushing the detectives to the edge physically and mentally. Eventually, John Doe turns himself in, but with a chilling twist—he will only reveal the location of his final two victims if the detectives accompany him. The journey leads to a shocking and devastating climax involving envy and wrath, forcing Mills into a tragic, irreversible decision. The film’s dark tone, atmospheric visuals, and moral complexity leave a lingering impact. It’s a haunting exploration of justice, morality, and the darkness within human nature.
