SPOTSTAR - DEIVA THIRUMAGAL

Deivathirumagal is a heartwarming Tamil drama that tells the emotional story of Krishna, a mentally challenged man with the mind of a child, who fights for the custody of his beloved daughter, Nila. Despite his limitations, Krishna is a loving and devoted father who raises Nila with care and joy. Their bond is pure and unbreakable, but society questions his ability to be a responsible parent. When circumstances force Nila’s relatives to take her away, Krishna embarks on a legal battle to win her back. Vikram delivers a deeply moving performance as Krishna, capturing both innocence and strength with remarkable sensitivity. Sara Arjun, as Nila, brings charm and natural emotion to the screen, making their relationship feel genuine and touching. Anushka Shetty plays the compassionate lawyer who takes up Krishna’s case, slowly coming to understand his world and his love for his daughter. The film explores themes of love, acceptance, and the definition of true parenthood. With gentle storytelling and emotional depth, it highlights how love doesn't need to be perfect to be powerful. Deivathirumagal is a poignant reminder of the strength of unconditional love and the courage found in the most unexpected hearts.
