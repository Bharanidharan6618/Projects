SPOTSTAR - CHIDAMBARAM

CHIDAMBARAM

Chidambaram is a rising Malayalam film director known for his grounded storytelling and unique cinematic voice. He made his directorial debut with the film Jan.E.Man in 2021, a dark comedy that balanced humor with emotional depth. The film was co-written with his brother Ganapathi, who also played one of the lead roles. Jan.E.Man received praise for its fresh narrative style and engaging characters. In 2024, Chidambaram directed Manjummel Boys, a survival thriller based on a real-life cave rescue involving a group of friends. The film became a massive success at the box office and was widely acclaimed for its gripping screenplay and technical finesse. It broke several records and became one of the highest-grossing Malayalam films ever. Chidambaram has a background in working as an assistant director and has collaborated with renowned cinematographers, which reflects in the visual style of his films. He is known for choosing stories rooted in local culture and human relationships. His ability to create suspense while maintaining emotional authenticity has made him a standout voice in new-generation Malayalam cinema. Chidambaram is also preparing to step into Hindi cinema with an upcoming gangster drama, showing his ambition to reach a wider audience. Despite offers to remake his films, he has expressed strong views about preserving cultural context and authenticity. His work is marked by sincerity, detail, and a deep connection to the people and places he portrays.
