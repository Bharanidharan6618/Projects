SPOTSTAR - ROMANCHAM

Romancham is a Malayalam horror-comedy set in Bengaluru during the early 2000s, revolving around a group of friends living together in a shared house. The story begins when they decide to experiment with an Ouija board out of boredom, hoping for some harmless fun. However, things take a bizarre turn as strange, unexplained incidents start occurring around them. What follows is a hilarious yet spooky series of events where fear and comedy constantly intertwine. Each friend reacts differently — some with disbelief, others with panic, and a few embracing the thrill. The film thrives on its natural humor, drawn from relatable situations and witty dialogues. While the supernatural element keeps the tension alive, it is the camaraderie and antics of the friends that form the heart of the story. The quirky personalities of the characters make every scene engaging and unpredictable. The pacing ensures both laughs and chills are evenly spread, keeping the audience hooked. In the end, Romancham becomes less about ghosts and more about friendship, human reactions, and how chaos can sometimes bring people closer.
