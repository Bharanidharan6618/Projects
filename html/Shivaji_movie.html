SPOTSTAR - SIVAJI

Sivaji: The Boss is a grand Tamil action drama directed by Shankar, featuring Rajinikanth in one of his most iconic roles. The film tells the story of Sivaji, a successful software systems architect who returns from the United States with a noble dream to build free education and healthcare systems for the underprivileged in India. However, his plans are blocked by corrupt politicians and greedy businessmen who see him as a threat to their power. Undeterred, Sivaji decides to fight the system using both brains and boldness, transforming himself into an unstoppable force. Rajinikanth delivers a magnetic performance, effortlessly balancing humor, style, and seriousness. Shriya Saran plays his love interest, adding warmth to the high-energy narrative. The film is filled with punchy dialogues, stylish visuals, and clever twists that keep the audience engaged. A. R. Rahman’s music adds glamour and energy, especially in the grand song sequences. The transformation of Sivaji into “MGR” mode marks a thrilling turn in the story. The film blends mass appeal with a strong social message about corruption and inequality. With its vibrant visuals and powerful storytelling, Sivaji became a landmark in commercial Tamil cinema. It showcases the charisma of Rajinikanth and the ambition of socially driven cinema.
