SPOTSTAR - LIFE IN A YEAR

Life in a Year is a heartfelt romantic drama about Daryn, a driven high school student with his future meticulously planned, and Isabelle, a free-spirited young woman who changes his life forever. When Daryn discovers that Isabelle is terminally ill and has only one year left to live, he decides to give her an entire lifetime’s worth of experiences within that single year. Together, they embark on adventures, chase dreams, and share moments of pure joy, love, and vulnerability. Isabelle teaches Daryn to break free from the rigid path laid out by his father and truly live in the moment. Their relationship blossoms with laughter, passion, and tenderness, but is also shadowed by the inevitable heartbreak of her illness. As time passes, they confront the fragility of life and the beauty of cherishing every second. The film captures the highs and lows of love, the courage it takes to embrace happiness despite pain, and the power of human connection. By the end, Daryn is transformed, carrying Isabelle’s spirit and lessons forward. It’s a moving reminder that love, even when fleeting, can leave an everlasting mark on the soul.
