SPOTSTAR - OHM SHANTHI OSHAANA

Ohm Shanti Oshana is a lighthearted and charming Malayalam romantic comedy told through the perspective of a teenage girl named Pooja. From the very start, Pooja is on a mission to find true love and refuses to settle for anything less. She decides that she will only marry someone she genuinely loves, which leads her to pursue Giri, a kind but unsuspecting local youth. Despite his initial resistance, Pooja’s playful persistence and innocence eventually win him over. The film balances teenage romance with gentle humor and emotional warmth while avoiding any crude elements. Nazriya Nazim shines in the lead role, bringing authenticity and energy to Pooja’s character. Nivin Pauly is steady and understated as Giri, perfectly complementing her performance. Supporting characters like the friends and family add humor and context without overshadowing the main story. The narrative remains breezy and affectionate, touching lightly on social themes such as expectations and identity. Scenes like a hair-pulling sequence underscore the fun innocence of teen love. Overall, it offers a refreshing take on young romance, grounded in simplicity and genuine emotion, making it a feel-good and memorable film ([lensmenreviews.com][1]).
