SPOTSTAR - 7 AUM ARIVU

7 Aum Arivu is a science fiction action film that blends history, technology, and martial arts into a gripping narrative. The story begins with the legend of Bodhidharma, a Tamil prince and monk who traveled to China and became the father of Shaolin martial arts and Chinese medicine. In the present day, a genetic engineering project led by a young scientist, Subha, aims to awaken Bodhidharma's skills and memory in one of his descendants, Aravind. Meanwhile, a skilled Chinese agent named Dong Lee is sent to India with a deadly mission involving bio-warfare. As Aravind slowly begins to unlock the hidden potential in his DNA, he becomes the only hope to stop the dangerous plan. Suriya plays both the ancient Bodhidharma and the modern-day Aravind with power and grace. Shruti Haasan impresses as the determined scientist who believes in history and science equally. The film explores the connection between the past and the future, tradition and innovation. Action scenes are well-crafted, blending martial arts with modern thrills. The background score and visuals enhance the intensity of the plot. 7 Aum Arivu encourages pride in cultural heritage while delivering a fast-paced, entertaining story. It stands out for its unique concept and ambitious execution.
