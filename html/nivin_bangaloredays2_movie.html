SPOTSTAR - BANGALORE DAYS

Bangalore Days follows the lives of three cousins from Kerala—Arjun, Divya, and Krishnan—who move to Bangalore in pursuit of dreams, love, and freedom. Arjun is a rebellious bike mechanic trying to escape his troubled past. Divya, newly married, struggles to find herself in a marriage overshadowed by emotional distance. Krishnan is a shy IT professional torn between tradition and modern life. The cousins share a deep bond and spend every moment together, finding comfort in each other’s company. Each cousin crosses paths with challenges that reshape their identities—Arjun falls in love with a radio jockey, Divya navigates marital strain, and Krishnan confronts heartbreak and nostalgia. Their relationships evolve with the city, which becomes almost a silent character offering liberation and opportunity. The film beautifully captures friendship, familial love, memory, and self-discovery through subtle moments and emotional honesty. Despite a long runtime, the pacing feels engaging with a gentle blend of humor and introspection. The ensemble performances feel natural and heartfelt, making you connect with their journeys. Bangalore Days remains celebrated for its portrayal of youth, ambition, and the emotional ties that define us.
