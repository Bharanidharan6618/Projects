SPOTSTAR - ONCE UPON A TIME IN HOLLYWOOD

Once Upon a Time in Hollywood is a drama-comedy set in 1969 Los Angeles, capturing the final moments of Hollywood’s golden age. It follows Rick Dalton, a fading television actor, and his stunt double and best friend, Cliff Booth, as they navigate a changing film industry. Rick struggles with his declining career, while Cliff drifts through life with his laid-back charm. Their stories intertwine with real-life Hollywood figures, including Sharon Tate, who represents the fresh, hopeful face of the industry. The film beautifully recreates the atmosphere of late 1960s LA, filled with neon lights, classic cars, and vintage film sets. Director Quentin Tarantino blends fictional characters with historical events, leading up to a tense and unpredictable climax. The pacing is relaxed, allowing time to explore the characters’ personalities and struggles. Humor and nostalgia run throughout, alongside moments of sudden intensity. In the end, it delivers both a love letter to the era and an alternate take on a tragic moment in history, leaving a lasting impression on viewers.
