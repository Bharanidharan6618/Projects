SPOTSTAR - SHRUTI HAASAN

SHRUTI HAASAN

Shruti Haasan is a multi-talented actress, singer, composer, and storyteller who has carved her own niche across Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and English cinema. Born into a cinematic lineage—her father Kamal Haasan and mother Sarika—she began her acting journey as a child artist and later debuted in Bollywood with Luck (2009), followed by her breakthrough in Telugu-Tamil films like 7aum Arivu and Gabbar Singh. Over the years, she has delivered memorable performances in Krack, Vakeel Saab, Veera Simha Reddy, Waltair Veerayya, and the pan-India action blockbuster Salaar: Part 1 – Ceasefire. In each role, she blends strong screen presence with emotional depth and musical talent. Besides acting, Shruti has also composed music and lent her voice to numerous songs, emphasizing the healing and spiritual connection she shares with music. 2023 marked her English-language debut with the psychological thriller The Eye, filmed in the UK and screened at international festivals, showcasing her versatility beyond Indian screens. The year 2025 has proved to be especially significant for her—she celebrated her 39th birthday with the team of Coolie, describing the year as “magical,” and expressed personal growth in becoming “bigger, better, stronger.” She has three major films lined up: Coolie with Rajinikanth, Train with Vijay Sethupathi, and Jana Nayagan with Vijay—demonstrating her strong presence in upcoming Tamil cinema, along with Salaar Part 2 in Telugu. Shruti continues to advocate for causes like mental health, gender parity, and financial independence, often using her platform and artistry to give voice to women’s rights. Known for her grounded, resilient spirit and artistic versatility, she remains one of South India’s most compelling and evolving cinematic talents.
