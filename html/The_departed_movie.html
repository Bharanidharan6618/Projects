SPOTSTAR - THE DEPARTED

The Departed is an intense crime thriller that weaves a tense game of deception between the police and the Irish mob in Boston. The story follows Billy Costigan, a young cop sent undercover to infiltrate the powerful crime syndicate run by Frank Costello. At the same time, Colin Sullivan, a trusted member of the police force, is secretly working for Costello as his mole. As both men operate in opposite worlds, each tries to uncover the identity of the other without blowing their own cover. The film builds a nerve-wracking atmosphere where loyalty is fragile, and every move could be deadly. Tensions escalate as clues surface, and both sides realize they have been infiltrated. The double lives take a toll, blurring the lines between right and wrong for both Billy and Colin. Trust becomes an impossible luxury, and betrayal lurks around every corner. When the truth begins to emerge, the story hurtles toward a violent and shocking climax. With its gripping pace, sharp dialogue, and unpredictable turns, The Departed explores the dangerous cost of living a lie in a world where survival depends on outsmarting everyone else.
