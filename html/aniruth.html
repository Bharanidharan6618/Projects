SPOTSTAR - ANIRUDH RAVICHANDER

ANIRUDH RAVICHANDER

Anirudh Ravichander is one of the most popular and influential music composers and playback singers in Indian cinema, especially known for his work in Tamil films. Born on October 16, 1990, in Chennai, he is the son of actor Ravi Raghavendra and the nephew of superstar Rajinikanth. He rose to instant fame with his debut composition "Why This Kolaveri Di" from the film 3, which became a global sensation and marked a new wave in Indian film music. Anirudh is known for his fresh sound, blending Indian classical elements with modern genres like EDM, pop, hip-hop, and rock. His ability to connect with youth and create chartbusters has made him a go-to composer in the industry. Some of his biggest hits include music from films like Vedalam, Thanga Magan, Master, Doctor, Vikram, and Jailer. He has also composed for Telugu and Hindi films, expanding his reach across India. Anirudh is not just a composer but also a talented singer, lending his energetic and emotional vocals to many of his own songs. His background in Western classical piano and Carnatic music gives his compositions both structure and soul. Despite his young age, he has worked with almost all top stars including Vijay, Rajinikanth, Kamal Haasan, and Shah Rukh Khan. His fanbase continues to grow with each release, making him a trendsetter in Indian film music. Known for his dedication, innovation, and stage presence, Anirudh has redefined the sound of modern South Indian cinema.
