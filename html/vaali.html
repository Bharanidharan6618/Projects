SPOTSTAR - VAALI

VAALI

Vaali was one of the most legendary and prolific lyricists in Tamil cinema, known for his poetic brilliance, versatility, and ability to connect with audiences across generations. Born as T.S. Rangarajan in 1931, he adopted the pen name Vaali and entered the film industry in the 1950s. His breakthrough came with the song "Sorgame Endralum" in the film Sumaithaangi, and from then on, he became an irreplaceable force in Tamil songwriting. Vaali wrote lyrics for more than 15,000 songs over a career spanning five decades, working with several generations of music directors and actors. His lyrics ranged from classical and philosophical to playful and romantic, always reflecting deep understanding and emotion. He was known for his collaborations with composers like M.S. Viswanathan, Ilaiyaraaja, and later A.R. Rahman. Vaali had a unique talent for using simple yet powerful words that resonated with people from all walks of life. His songs have been featured in timeless films such as Billa, Sathya, Aboorva Sagodharargal, and Sivaji. Even in his later years, he adapted effortlessly to changing trends and remained relevant in modern cinema. Apart from film lyrics, he also wrote poetry, books, and dialogues, showcasing his literary depth. Vaali was honored with numerous awards, including the Padma Shri, for his contribution to arts and literature. His legacy lives on through his unforgettable songs that continue to move, inspire, and entertain.
