SPOTSTAR - TIK TIK TIK

Tik Tik Tik is a rare Tamil space thriller centered on a mission to prevent an asteroid from colliding with Earth. The story starts when scientists discover that a massive asteroid will strike near Chennai in just seven days. A special team, including an escape artist named Vasu, two astronauts, a hacker, and a lieutenant, is assembled to space‑infiltrate and retrieve a nuclear missile from a foreign station. Vasu, played by Jayam Ravi, brings lighthearted charm and magic tricks into this high-stakes rescue mission. They must launch the missile to obliterate the asteroid before time runs out. The film balances tense moments of peril with emotional stakes, particularly the conflict of saving Vasu’s kidnapped son. The visual effects, especially in the space sequences, are impressive considering the modest budget. Critics praised the ambitious attempt at Indian sci‑fi, though many noted some logical inconsistencies and plot shortcuts. The ensemble cast contributes solid performances, with Vinoth and Nivetha adding depth to the mission drama. The narrative moves from urgency and suspense to heartfelt drama as the countdown ticks. Despite flaws in realism, the movie delivers a mass‑appeal experience wrapped in futuristic visuals and patriotic zeal. Tik Tik Tik may not fit hard‑core sci‑fi standards, but it fulfills its promise as a locally rooted space adventure. It remains India’s first Tamil film set partly beyond earth, offering novelty and entertainment.
