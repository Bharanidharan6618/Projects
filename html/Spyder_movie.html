SPOTSTAR - SPYDER

Spyder is a bilingual action thriller that centers around an intelligent and emotionally driven protagonist, Shiva, played by Mahesh Babu. He works for the Intelligence Bureau and uses technology to secretly help people in distress. One day, while tracking a distress call, he stumbles upon a gruesome crime that leads him to a psychotic killer who thrives on people's pain and fear. As Shiva digs deeper, the cat-and-mouse game begins between him and the antagonist, played chillingly by S. J. Suryah. The film explores the thin line between justice and personal morality, set against a backdrop of high-stakes tension. Directed by A. R. Murugadoss, Spyder combines psychological drama with action, keeping the audience engaged throughout. Rakul Preet Singh plays the female lead, adding lighter moments and emotional balance. The story’s uniqueness lies in its antagonist, whose twisted motives are rooted in his disturbing childhood. The visuals are sleek, and the suspense builds steadily with each revelation. Harris Jayaraj’s music and background score heighten the film’s intensity. Spyder stands out for its blend of technology, action, and psychological thrills, making it a gripping cinematic experience.
