SPOTSTAR - JAILER

Jailer is a gripping Tamil action thriller that marks the powerful return of Rajinikanth in a commanding role. The film revolves around Muthuvel Pandian, a retired jailer who leads a peaceful life until a personal tragedy forces him back into a world of violence and justice. When his son, a sincere police officer, goes missing during an investigation into an organ trafficking network, Muthuvel is pushed to uncover the truth. As he delves deeper, he reveals a darker and more ruthless side of himself that had long been hidden. Rajinikanth delivers a charismatic performance, blending calm wisdom with fierce intensity. The film is packed with stylish action, emotional depth, and sharp dialogues that resonate with the audience. Director Nelson crafts a tight narrative with unexpected twists and a strong supporting cast. The background score by Anirudh adds to the film’s tension and heroism. Jailer explores themes of justice, fatherhood, and moral conflict, highlighting the strength of a man who will go to any lengths for his family. The film balances mass appeal with emotional storytelling, making it both entertaining and impactful. With its intense moments and strong performances, Jailer stands out as a memorable action drama.
