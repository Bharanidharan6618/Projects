SPOTSTAR - AYYAPPANUM KOSHIYUM

Ayyapanum Koshiyum is a powerful Malayalam action-drama that explores ego, pride, and power struggles between two strong-willed men. The story revolves around Koshy Kurien, a wealthy and influential ex-army officer, and Ayyappan Nair, a principled and fearless police officer. Their paths cross when Koshy is arrested for transporting liquor through a dry area, sparking a chain of confrontations that escalate into a battle of dominance. What begins as a legal issue soon transforms into a deeply personal feud fueled by male ego and a refusal to back down. The film presents both characters with equal weight, never portraying one as entirely right or wrong. Their rivalry unfolds in a realistic and intense manner, capturing the unpredictability of human behavior. The dialogues are sharp and loaded with emotion, making each encounter gripping. As the story progresses, it raises questions about masculinity, justice, and redemption. The backdrop of rural Kerala adds authenticity and texture to the narrative. Ayyapanum Koshiyum is not just an action film but a layered character study, making it a thought-provoking and engaging experience.
