SPOTSTAR - ARANMANAI 4

Aranmanai 4 is a Tamil horror-comedy film that continues the popular franchise known for blending supernatural elements with humor. The story follows Saravanan, who returns to his ancestral home after the mysterious death of his sister. As he investigates, he uncovers dark secrets hidden within the mansion, including the presence of a vengeful spirit known as Baak. The film cleverly uses suspense and comedy to maintain a balance between scares and laughs. Sundar C plays the lead role with a mix of seriousness and comic timing, while Tamannaah and Raashii Khanna bring depth to their characters. The supporting cast adds flavor with well-timed humor and energetic performances. The mansion setting enhances the eerie atmosphere, filled with sudden twists and haunted visuals. The background music heightens the mood during intense scenes without becoming overwhelming. The horror sequences are crafted with engaging visuals, and the emotional subplot adds weight to the story. The movie follows the usual formula of the Aranmanai series but still manages to entertain with its engaging narrative and fun elements. It offers a family-friendly horror experience with strong visuals and light-hearted moments. Aranmanai 4 is a satisfying entry for fans of the series and Tamil horror-comedies in general.
