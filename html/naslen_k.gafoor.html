SPOTSTAR - NASLEN K.GAFOOR

NASLEN K.GAFOOR

Naslen K Gafoor is a young and promising actor in the Malayalam film industry, known for his natural charm and effortless humor. He was born on June 11, 2000, in Kodungallur, Kerala. Naslen made his debut with Thanneer Mathan Dinangal in 2019, where his portrayal of Melvin was instantly loved by audiences. His witty dialogues and subtle expressions set him apart as a fresh face with immense potential. He continued to shine in films like Kuruthi, Home, and Super Sharanya, each role showing different shades of his talent. In 2022 and 2023, he appeared in popular films like Jo and Jo and Neymar, both of which strengthened his place in the industry. His big breakthrough as a lead came with the 2024 romantic comedy Premalu, where his role as Sachin won hearts across Kerala and beyond. The film was a massive hit and brought Naslen widespread recognition. Following this, he played the lead in Alappuzha Gymkhana, a sports comedy where he portrayed a boxer with grit and humor. Despite his rising fame, Naslen remains humble and focused on his craft. He is known for bringing relatable, grounded characters to life without overacting. With several exciting projects ahead, including a sequel to Premalu, he continues to be one of the most exciting young talents in Malayalam cinema.
