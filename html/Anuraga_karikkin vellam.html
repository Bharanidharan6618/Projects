SPOTSTAR - ANURAGA KARIKKIN VELLAM

Anuraga Karikkin Vellam is a heartfelt Malayalam film that beautifully portrays love, relationships, and family dynamics across generations. The story follows Abhilash, a young police officer navigating a complicated relationship with his girlfriend Elizabeth, while also observing changes in his parents' long-standing marriage. What sets the film apart is its grounded storytelling and the way it captures the little emotions of daily life. Abhilash’s father, Raghu, begins to reflect on a woman from his past, which leads to a series of events that reconnect the family in unexpected ways. The film balances humor and emotion effortlessly, giving space for each character to grow. Elizabeth’s journey after the breakup is especially poignant, showing her evolution into a stronger and more self-aware individual. The father-son dynamics, once strained, start to shift as both begin to understand each other better. The film does not rely on melodrama but speaks through its simplicity and sincerity. Set against the backdrop of middle-class life, the narrative is relatable and honest. Anuraga Karikkin Vellam leaves a lasting impression through its gentle storytelling and the warmth of everyday love.
