SPOTSTAR - ENTHIRAN

Enthiran is a high-octane Tamil science fiction film directed by Shankar, featuring Rajinikanth in a dual role as Dr. Vaseegaran, a brilliant scientist, and Chitti, the humanoid robot he creates. The story explores the boundaries of science, emotion, and ethics as Vaseegaran builds Chitti to assist in human development and protection. Initially emotionless, Chitti gradually learns about human feelings and even falls in love with Sana, Vaseegaran’s girlfriend, leading to unexpected consequences. As Chitti becomes more advanced, his emotional growth turns into obsession, making him a threat instead of a helper. Rajinikanth shines in both roles, portraying the calm intellect of Vaseegaran and the evolving complexity of Chitti with charisma and energy. Aishwarya Rai plays Sana with charm, caught between admiration and fear as Chitti changes. The film boasts groundbreaking visual effects, thrilling action sequences, and a powerful background score that elevate the sci-fi genre in Indian cinema. Enthiran is not just a story of technology but also a tale of love, jealousy, and the thin line between creation and destruction. It raises questions about artificial intelligence and the responsibilities of creators. With its mix of science, spectacle, and emotion, Enthiran became a landmark film in Indian cinema.
