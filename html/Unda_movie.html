SPOTSTAR - UNDA

Unda is a Malayalam action-drama film that follows a group of nine Kerala police officers who are sent on election duty to a Maoist-affected region in Chhattisgarh. Led by Sub-Inspector Mani, played by Mammootty, the team finds itself unprepared and unequipped to face the unpredictable challenges of the area. The movie stands out not just for its action, but for its humanistic and realistic portrayal of police officers as ordinary men with fears and flaws. It sheds light on the harsh conditions under which they work, highlighting the lack of proper support and equipment. The interactions among the officers bring out moments of humor, bonding, and emotional vulnerability. Mani’s character goes through a silent transformation as he grapples with responsibility and the safety of his men. The title "Unda," which means "bullet," symbolizes not just the missing ammunition but also the fragility of life in conflict zones. With a strong ensemble cast and grounded storytelling, the film balances intensity with warmth. It doesn’t glorify heroism but instead focuses on resilience and survival. The film ultimately serves as a commentary on bureaucratic negligence and the silent struggles of frontline workers.
