SPOTSTAR - TENET

Tenet is a high-concept science fiction thriller that explores the manipulation of time in a race to prevent global catastrophe. The story follows a skilled but unnamed protagonist recruited by a secretive organization to stop a looming threat worse than nuclear war. He is introduced to the concept of “inversion,” where objects and people can move backward through time while the rest of the world moves forward. Partnering with an enigmatic ally named Neil, he navigates a maze of espionage, betrayal, and shifting loyalties. The antagonist, a ruthless arms dealer named Sator, has the ability to communicate with the future and plans to end the world if he cannot have it. The film’s narrative constantly plays with cause and effect, making the audience rethink the flow of events. Through complex action sequences and stunning visuals, time itself becomes both the weapon and the battlefield. The protagonist must learn to think beyond linear time to succeed. In the end, the truth about Neil, the mission, and the protagonist’s future role adds a poignant twist to the intricate puzzle.
