SPOTSTAR - OPPENHEIMER

Oppenheimer is an epic biographical drama that explores the life of J Robert Oppenheimer, the brilliant theoretical physicist who played a central role in developing the atomic bomb during World War II. Directed by Christopher Nolan, the film captures the intense journey of scientific ambition, moral conflict, and political intrigue. It follows Oppenheimer’s rise as a key figure in the Manhattan Project, working with a team of scientists to create a weapon that would change the course of history. As the project nears completion, he is forced to confront the ethical weight of his work and the devastating consequences of nuclear warfare. The film masterfully blends personal struggles with larger geopolitical tensions, showing how genius can be both a gift and a burden. It delves into themes of power, responsibility, and the price of progress. The narrative moves between timelines, weaving scientific breakthroughs with intense moments of political scrutiny. Cillian Murphy delivers a deeply layered portrayal of Oppenheimer, supported by a stellar ensemble cast. With its gripping storytelling and thought-provoking subject matter, the movie leaves audiences questioning the balance between innovation and morality.
