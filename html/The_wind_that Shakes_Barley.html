SPOTSTAR - THE WIND THAT SHAKES THE BARLEY

The Wind That Shakes the Barley is a gripping historical drama set in Ireland during the 1920s, against the backdrop of the Irish War of Independence and the subsequent Civil War. The story follows two brothers, Damien and Teddy, who initially share the same dream of fighting for Ireland’s freedom from British rule. Damien, a young doctor, is drawn into the conflict after witnessing the brutality of the occupying forces. Together with Teddy, he joins the Irish Republican Army, engaging in guerrilla warfare to reclaim their country’s independence. However, after a peace treaty is signed, divisions emerge among the revolutionaries over its terms. The brothers find themselves on opposite sides of the political divide, turning their unity into a heartbreaking rivalry. The film vividly portrays the sacrifices, moral dilemmas, and personal costs of war. Through its emotional storytelling and powerful performances, it shows how ideals can be tested by political compromise. The rural landscapes contrast sharply with the violence and tension of the struggle. Ultimately, it’s a poignant exploration of loyalty, family, and the heavy price of freedom.
