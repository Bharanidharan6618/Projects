SPOTSTAR - DOLITTLE

Dolittle is a fantasy adventure film about Dr. John Dolittle, a brilliant but eccentric veterinarian who has the unique ability to talk to animals. After losing his wife in a tragic accident, Dolittle isolates himself from the world, living only with his animal companions in a secluded estate. His peaceful life is interrupted when Queen Victoria falls gravely ill, and he is called upon to find a rare cure. To save her, Dolittle embarks on an epic journey across dangerous seas to a mysterious island in search of a magical healing fruit. Along the way, his loyal animal friends — including a nervous gorilla, a witty parrot, and a playful polar bear — help him face numerous challenges. The adventure is filled with humor, heart, and breathtaking landscapes. Through the journey, Dolittle learns to open his heart again and reconnect with people. The movie celebrates friendship, courage, and the bond between humans and animals.
