SPOTSTAR - ANNIYAN

Anniyan is a psychological action thriller that combines intense drama with a strong social message. The story revolves around Ramanujam, a sincere and law-abiding lawyer who is deeply frustrated by the widespread corruption and apathy in society. His inner turmoil leads to the emergence of a split personality named Anniyan, a ruthless vigilante who punishes wrongdoers in extreme ways. Vikram delivers a brilliant performance, effortlessly switching between the calm and soft-spoken Ramanujam, the stylish model Remo, and the terrifying Anniyan. The film explores the mental and emotional breakdown of a man who feels helpless in the face of injustice. Directed by Shankar, it uses gripping visuals, powerful music, and creative storytelling to highlight the importance of personal and social responsibility. The character of Anniyan becomes a symbol of suppressed anger that many people feel in a flawed system. Sadha plays Nandini, Ramanujam’s love interest, who slowly uncovers the truth behind his changing behavior. The film blends action, romance, suspense, and psychological depth in a way that keeps the audience engaged throughout. Anniyan raises questions about morality, justice, and the cost of silence. It remains one of Tamil cinema’s most iconic films for its bold theme and exceptional execution.
