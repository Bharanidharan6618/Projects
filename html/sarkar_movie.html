SPOTSTAR - SARKAR

Sarkar is a political action drama that follows the journey of Sundar Ramasamy, a successful NRI businessman who returns to India to cast his vote, only to find that his vote has been illegally used. Shocked and angered by the corruption in the electoral system, Sundar decides to take matters into his own hands. Using his intelligence, resources, and influence, he challenges the political system and decides to run for office, inspiring the youth to rise against injustice. Vijay delivers a powerful performance as Sundar, portraying both charisma and conviction. The film, directed by A. R. Murugadoss, highlights issues such as electoral fraud, political manipulation, and the need for systemic change. Keerthy Suresh plays the female lead, offering support and emotional grounding to the story. The film’s dialogues are punchy and filled with strong political undertones. A. R. Rahman’s music adds intensity to the narrative and uplifts key moments. Sarkar is not just an action film but a call for awakening, aimed at encouraging responsible citizenship and youth involvement in politics. With sharp storytelling and a relevant message, the film makes a strong impact.
