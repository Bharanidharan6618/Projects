SPOTSTAR - STREE

Stree is a unique blend of horror and comedy set in a small town in India, where a mysterious spirit abducts men during a local festival. The townspeople live in fear, writing messages on their walls to keep the spirit away. The story follows Vicky, a talented tailor with a quiet charm, who becomes curious about a strange woman he meets during the celebrations. As more men go missing, suspicion grows, and Vicky and his friends decide to uncover the truth behind the legend of Stree. The film balances spooky moments with clever humor, making it both entertaining and eerie. Rajkummar Rao shines as Vicky, bringing a perfect mix of innocence and bravery to the role. Shraddha Kapoor plays the mysterious woman with an enigmatic presence. The supporting cast adds charm and laughter, creating memorable moments throughout the film. Stree subtly addresses gender dynamics, flipping traditional roles in a smart and thought-provoking way. The film’s atmosphere, dialogue, and pacing keep the audience engaged from start to finish. It delivers scares without relying on clichés, making it stand out in the horror-comedy genre. Stree became a surprise hit, praised for its originality and strong performances. It ends on an intriguing note, leaving room for deeper interpretation and discussion.
