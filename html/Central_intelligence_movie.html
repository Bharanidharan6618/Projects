SPOTSTAR - CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE

Central Intelligence is an action-comedy about Calvin Joyner, a mild-mannered accountant whose quiet life takes a wild turn when he reconnects with Bob Stone, a former high school classmate. Back in school, Bob was overweight and bullied, but now he’s a muscular, highly skilled CIA agent. Bob pulls Calvin into a dangerous mission involving stolen government secrets, claiming that Calvin’s accounting skills are crucial to stopping a deadly threat. As the two navigate shootouts, chases, and betrayals, Calvin struggles to figure out if Bob is really a hero or if he’s hiding something. Their mismatched personalities — Calvin’s cautious nature and Bob’s over-the-top enthusiasm — lead to plenty of comedic situations. Along the way, the film explores friendship, trust, and the courage to step out of one’s comfort zone. With a mix of action-packed sequences and laugh-out-loud moments, the movie delivers an entertaining ride where unlikely partners prove they can make a great team.
