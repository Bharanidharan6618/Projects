SPOTSTAR - Dr.DOLITTLE

Dr Dolittle is a lighthearted and adventurous story about a man who has the rare gift of talking to animals. After the death of his beloved wife, he isolates himself from the world, living only with his collection of animal friends in his grand yet hidden estate. His quiet life changes when Queen Victoria falls seriously ill, and Dolittle is summoned to find a legendary cure. He sets sail on a daring journey across the seas, joined by a colorful crew of animals including a timid gorilla, a feisty parrot, a witty polar bear, and many more. Along the way, they encounter dangerous obstacles, rival explorers, and thrilling adventures that test their courage. Dolittle slowly opens his heart again, finding healing through his bond with his companions. The film blends humor, action, and heartfelt moments, creating a story full of wonder and warmth. It celebrates friendship, bravery, and the idea that kindness can bridge any gap — even between humans and animals.
