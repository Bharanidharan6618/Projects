SID SRIRAM

Sid Sriram is a renowned Indian-American playback singer, Carnatic vocalist, music producer, and songwriter known for his unique blend of classical and contemporary styles. Born on May 19, 1990, in Chennai and raised in California, he began learning Carnatic music at the age of three under his mother, Latha Sriram. Growing up, he was equally influenced by R\&B, soul, and gospel music, which shaped his distinctive sound. He studied music production and engineering at the prestigious Berklee College of Music. Sid gained widespread recognition with his debut song "Adiye" in the Tamil film *Kadal*, composed by A. R. Rahman. Since then, he has delivered numerous chart-topping hits in Tamil, Telugu, and other South Indian languages, including “Ennodu Nee Irundhaal,” “Thalli Pogathey,” “Samajavaragamana,” and “Srivalli.” His voice is known for its emotional depth and ability to move listeners with raw, soulful expression. Sid’s music often combines intricate Carnatic ornamentation with modern rhythm and texture. He has received several Filmfare Awards and is one of the most sought-after singers in Indian cinema. In addition to film songs, he has released independent English albums like *Entropy* and *Sidharth*, which showcase his genre-blending artistry. His global performances, including at NPR’s Tiny Desk and Coachella, reflect his growing international reach. Despite fame, he remains grounded and sees music as a spiritual experience. Sid Sriram continues to break boundaries, inspiring millions with his authentic voice and cross-cultural musical vision.
