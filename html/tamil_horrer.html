HORROR

Tamil horror movies have grown significantly over the years, blending supernatural elements with emotional and psychological depth. Early films focused on ghost stories and haunted mansions, while modern horror explores more layered themes like trauma, revenge, and social issues. Movies like Pizza, Maya, Aval, and Pisasu have redefined the genre with strong storytelling and atmospheric visuals. Horror-comedy has also become popular, attracting wider audiences with films like Aranmanai and Kanchana. Today, Tamil horror stands out for its unique mix of fear, emotion, and cultural relevance. The genre continues to evolve with fresh talent and creative direction.
