SPOTSTAR - DD RETURNS

DD Returns is a Tamil horror comedy film starring Santhanam in the lead role. It is the third installment in the Dhilluku Dhuddu series and blends supernatural elements with humor. The story follows a group of people who enter a haunted mansion to retrieve hidden money but end up trapped in a deadly game run by ghosts. Santhanam’s character brings comic relief while also navigating the eerie challenges inside the house. The screenplay cleverly balances jump scares and hilarious moments. The film’s strength lies in its timing, fast-paced editing, and strong supporting performances. Characters like Redin Kingsley and Munishkanth add to the comedy with their quirky mannerisms. The haunted house setup is creatively used for slapstick and situational comedy. Despite being a horror film, the tone remains light-hearted, making it suitable for a family audience. The second half of the movie builds up the tension while never losing its comedic flavor. Director Prem Anand keeps the plot engaging without overcomplicating it. The film was well-received for avoiding clichés and offering fresh humor. DD Returns stands out as an entertaining mix of horror, thrills, and laughter.
