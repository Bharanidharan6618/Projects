SPOTSTAR - GODHA

Godha is a refreshing Malayalam sports drama that centers around wrestling, dreams, and empowerment. The story follows Aditi, a passionate wrestler from Punjab, who flees to Kerala to escape familial pressures. There, she meets Anjaneya Das, a carefree young man whose father, Captain, runs a wrestling academy. As Aditi trains and inspires others around her, she also finds herself facing challenges rooted in gender bias and societal expectations. The film beautifully explores themes of perseverance, self-respect, and the breaking of stereotypes. It’s not just about sports but also about the journey of self-discovery and standing up for one’s passion. Aditi’s strength, both physical and emotional, drives the narrative forward with intensity and heart. The rural setting and the clash of cultures between north and south India add an interesting layer to the story. Music, humor, and well-executed action sequences keep the film engaging. Godha ultimately becomes a celebration of women’s empowerment and personal growth.
