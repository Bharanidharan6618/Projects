SPOTSTAR - BEAUTY AND THE BEAST

Beauty and the Beast is a timeless romantic fantasy that tells the story of Belle, a bright and independent young woman living in a small village. She dreams of adventure and a life beyond her provincial town. When her father is taken prisoner by a mysterious Beast in an enchanted castle, Belle bravely offers herself in his place. Over time, she discovers that the Beast is not merely a monster, but a cursed prince trapped by a powerful spell. The castle is filled with enchanted objects, each once human, who hope Belle might be the one to break the curse. As Belle and the Beast spend time together, their relationship grows from hostility to friendship, and eventually into love. The Beast learns kindness, humility, and selflessness, while Belle sees beyond appearances to the goodness within him. Meanwhile, the arrogant hunter Gaston plots to win Belle’s hand and destroy the Beast. In a climactic battle, true love’s kiss breaks the curse, restoring the prince and his servants to their human forms. The film is a magical story of compassion, transformation, and the belief that beauty lies within.
