SPOTSTAR - BALLERINA

Ballerina follows Eve Macarro, a former ballerina whose life is shattered when she witnesses her father’s brutal murder and is thrust into a hidden world of assassins. Raised and trained in the deadly school of the Ruska Roma under the enigmatic guidance of “The Director,” Eve becomes a precise and lethal agent. Consumed by grief and vengeance, she sets out to track down the killer amid the fierce underbelly of the assassin community. The film’s narrative unfolds between the events of John Wick: Chapter 3 and Chapter 4, blending hyper-stylised action with emotional motivation. Along her quest, familiar figures from the John Wick world—like Winston and the Continental staff—make appearances, grounding her journey in that universe. The choreography blends balletic grace with brutal combat, emphasizing Eve’s deadly precision. As she fights through waves of adversaries, the line between art and violence blurs. Beneath the flashy action lies a story of loss, purpose, and reclaiming identity in a world built on blood. The film closes with a dramatic showdown in a snow-dusted village of assassins, where Eve confronts the culmination of her grief with steel and resolve.
