SPOTSTAR - NERAM

Neram is a crisp and clever bilingual thriller (Malayalam and Tamil) that weaves comedy and tension into a tight narrative. The story revolves around Mathew, an IT professional struggling to regain his job, and his girlfriend, who finds herself in a dangerous situation involving debt and extortion. Over a single intense day, their paths intersect unpredictably, forcing both of them into a race against time. Aparna plays Monisha, the modern young woman caught between survival and rescue, while Nivin Pauly brings vulnerability and relatability to Mathew’s character. Director Alphonse Puthren crafts the screenplay with biting humor, smart coincidences, and well-timed suspense. The narrative cleverly connects seemingly unrelated events through friendship, desperation, and timing. The minimalist soundtrack and city visuals heighten the everyday urgency without overwhelming the story. Supporting characters inject both depth and humor, especially in moments of frustration and panic. The pace remains brisk, with enough emotional stakes to keep the audience invested. Neram stands out for its originality, sharp dialogue, and tight editing. It succeeds as both an entertaining thriller and a slice-of-life snapshot of youthful challenges.
