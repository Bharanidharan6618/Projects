SPOTSTAR - HANGOVER

The Hangover is a wild comedy that follows a group of friends who travel to Las Vegas for a bachelor party that spirals completely out of control. The next morning, they wake up in a trashed hotel suite with no memory of the previous night and the groom missing. As they scramble to piece together what happened, they uncover a series of outrageous events involving a stolen tiger, a missing tooth, and an unexpected baby in their care. Their search takes them through bizarre encounters with gangsters, the police, and even a famous celebrity. Each clue leads to even more absurd situations, testing their friendship and sanity. The movie blends mystery with over-the-top humor, keeping audiences laughing as the pieces of the night slowly come together. At its core, it’s about loyalty, friendship, and dealing with the crazy consequences of bad decisions. The Hangover became a cultural hit, known for its unpredictable twists, outrageous characters, and unforgettable comedic moments.
