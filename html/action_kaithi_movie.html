SPOTSTAR - KAITHI

Kaithi is a gripping Tamil action-thriller centered on Dilli, a recently released prisoner who just wants to meet his daughter for the first time. His plans are interrupted when a police officer enlists his help to save a group of cops poisoned by a drug syndicate. With no phones, no modern communication, and a ticking clock, Dilli must drive a truck loaded with the unconscious officers through dangerous territory swarming with armed gangsters. The night turns into a relentless chase filled with intense action sequences, clever tactics, and emotional moments. Dilli, despite his violent past, emerges as a man driven by love and responsibility rather than revenge. The film’s tension is heightened by its real-time narrative and absence of songs, keeping the focus on survival. Kaithi combines raw action with deep emotional stakes, making it a memorable story of sacrifice, redemption, and the lengths one will go to protect others.
