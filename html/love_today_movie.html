SPOTSTAR - LOVE TODAY

Love Today is a Tamil romantic comedy film that explores modern relationships in the digital age. The story revolves around a young couple, Pradeep and Nikitha, who are deeply in love and plan to get married. However, Nikitha's father proposes a unique condition before approving their relationship — they must exchange their mobile phones for one day. This condition leads to unexpected twists as both of them uncover secrets and insecurities hidden in their digital lives. What starts as a test of love soon becomes a rollercoaster of emotions filled with drama, humor, and misunderstandings. Pradeep Ranganathan, who also directed the film, plays the lead role with charm and relatability. Ivana portrays Nikitha with grace, balancing innocence and emotional strength. The film highlights how trust can be challenged by the transparency demanded in today’s relationships. Supporting characters, including family members, add depth and comic relief. With vibrant music and a fast-paced narrative, the movie keeps the audience engaged throughout. It cleverly portrays how technology both connects and divides people. The story feels modern, relatable, and thought-provoking. Love Today stands out for its fresh concept and strong emotional core.
