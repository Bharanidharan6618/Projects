SPOTSTAR - 28 DAYS LATER

28 Days Later is a post-apocalyptic horror thriller that redefined the zombie genre with its raw and realistic approach. The story begins when a highly contagious rage-inducing virus escapes from a laboratory, spreading rapidly across the United Kingdom. Jim, a bicycle courier, wakes up from a coma in an abandoned hospital to find London eerily deserted and society collapsed. As he wanders through the empty streets, he encounters survivors who explain the terrifying reality of the infected—humans transformed into violent, mindless predators. Together, they embark on a dangerous journey in search of safety, facing both the infected and desperate uninfected humans. The film captures the breakdown of civilization and the primal instincts that emerge when survival is at stake. It blends moments of quiet emptiness with bursts of intense, chaotic violence. Danny Boyle’s gritty direction and the haunting visuals give the story an unsettling realism. The characters are forced to confront not only the horror outside but also the darkness within humanity itself. Tense, emotional, and thought-provoking, 28 Days Later remains one of the most influential horror films of modern cinema.
