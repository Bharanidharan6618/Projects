SPOTSTAR - I

"I" is a visually stunning Tamil romantic thriller directed by Shankar, featuring Vikram in a transformative role that showcases his dedication and versatility. The story follows Lingesan, a humble bodybuilder from Chennai who dreams of becoming Mr. India. His life takes a dramatic turn when he becomes a top model under the name Lee and falls in love with Diya, a leading model in the industry. Just as success and love begin to bloom, Lingesan’s world shatters when he is mysteriously transformed into a disfigured man due to a cruel conspiracy. Betrayal, jealousy, and revenge drive the plot as he sets out to uncover those responsible for destroying his life. Vikram delivers a powerful performance, playing multiple physical transformations with incredible commitment. Amy Jackson plays Diya with elegance and strength, standing by Lingesan through his ups and downs. The film combines romance, action, and emotional drama with a unique blend of fantasy and science. A. R. Rahman’s music adds depth to the film’s emotional and visual highs. With grand sets, technical brilliance, and strong storytelling, "I" explores themes of inner beauty, resilience, and vengeance. It’s a tale of how love and determination can endure even the most brutal transformations.
