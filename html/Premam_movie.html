SPOTSTAR - PREMAM

Premam is a Malayalam romantic drama that beautifully explores the phases of love in a young man's life. Directed by Alphonse Puthren, the film follows George, an ordinary guy whose journey from teenage infatuation to mature love is portrayed with charm and realism. The movie is divided into three parts, each representing a different phase in George's romantic life — school crush, college romance, and adult love. Nivin Pauly delivers a natural and endearing performance as George. Sai Pallavi, in her debut role as Malar, became an instant favorite among audiences for her grace and simplicity. The film's strength lies in its relatability, humor, and emotional depth. Its simple storytelling, nostalgic visuals, and soulful music struck a chord with viewers across all age groups. The chemistry between the lead characters feels genuine and unforced. Supporting actors like Madonna Sebastian and Anupama Parameswaran add layers to the emotional fabric of the film. The cinematography captures the beauty of everyday life in Kerala. Rajesh Murugesan's music became a sensation, perfectly complementing the film's mood. Premam is not just a love story, but a celebration of love’s innocence, pain, and transformation. It continues to be remembered as one of the most beloved Malayalam films of its time.
