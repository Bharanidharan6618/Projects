SPOTSTAR - LUCIFER

Lucifer is a gripping political thriller that marks the directorial debut of actor Prithviraj Sukumaran, featuring Mohanlal in a powerful lead role. The story begins with the death of a prominent political leader, triggering a fierce power struggle within the party and unveiling the hidden corruption that drives politics. Mohanlal plays Stephen Nedumpally, a mysterious and influential figure whose past and identity slowly unravel as the plot thickens. His calm yet commanding presence becomes central to the unfolding drama, which delves into the nexus between politics, crime, and religion. The film cleverly balances intense action sequences with emotional depth and strong character arcs. As alliances shift and betrayals unfold, the narrative keeps the audience guessing. Lucifer is packed with political symbolism, sharp dialogues, and grand visuals, creating an immersive cinematic experience. Supporting performances from actors like Vivek Oberoi and Tovino Thomas add further weight to the story. The film explores themes of legacy, morality, and the cost of power. With stylish direction and a tightly woven screenplay, Lucifer delivers both mass appeal and thoughtful storytelling. It stands as a landmark film in Malayalam cinema, leaving room for an even bigger story ahead.
