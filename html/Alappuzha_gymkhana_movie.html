SPOTSTAR - ALAPPUZHA GYMKHANA

Alappuzha Gymkhana is a Malayalam sports drama filled with humor, heart, and raw energy. The story centers on Jojo, a 19-year-old who's adrift in life after failing his school exams. Along with his quirky friends, Jojo stumbles into the world of boxing as a last‑ditch effort to secure a spot in college through the sports quota. Under the tough guidance of Coach Joshua, these unlikely athletes begin to learn what discipline and purpose really mean. The film’s charm lies in its authenticity and grounded portrayal of youthful ambition. Naslen delivers a vibrant and emotional performance as Jojo, bringing both charm and vulnerability to the role. Lukman Avaran, Baby Jean, and the ensemble cast add warmth, humor, and relatability to every scene. The boxing sequences are visceral and real, merging physical grit with personal growth. Rooted in its coastal setting, the film balances the spectacle of sport with quiet emotional payoffs. It explores friendship, determination, and the beauty of starting over. Alappuzha Gymkhana became a runaway success thanks to its memorable characters and inspiring narrative. It’s a film that punches above its weight, leaving an impact long after the final round.
