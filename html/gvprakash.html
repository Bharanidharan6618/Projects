G.V.PRAKASH KUMAR

G.V. Prakash Kumar is a talented Indian music composer, playback singer, actor, and producer who primarily works in Tamil cinema. Born on June 13, 1987, in Chennai, he is the nephew of legendary composer A.R. Rahman. He started his career in music at a young age and gained recognition with his debut as a composer in the film Veyil, which received critical acclaim. His breakthrough came with the film Aadukalam, which featured chart-topping songs and won national recognition. G.V. Prakash is known for his catchy tunes, youthful energy, and ability to mix folk, classical, and contemporary styles effortlessly. Apart from composing, he has lent his voice to many hit songs and is admired for his unique vocal texture. Expanding his talent beyond music, he made his acting debut with the film Darling and later starred in several successful movies like Trisha Illana Nayanthara, Naachiyaar, and Ayngaran. He has carved a distinct identity in both music and acting, gaining a loyal fan following. G.V. Prakash is appreciated for taking up socially relevant scripts and portraying intense characters with depth. He has also produced films under his home banner, showing his interest in nurturing new content. Despite juggling multiple roles in the industry, he continues to deliver fresh and engaging work. His passion, versatility, and youthful appeal make him one of the most dynamic personalities in Tamil cinema today.
