SPOTSTAR - NEYMAR

Neymar is a Malayalam comedy-drama that centers around a mischievous dog named Neymar and the chaos he brings into the lives of two friends, Kunjava and Sinto. The story begins when Kunjava impulsively buys the dog, naming it after the famous footballer. What starts as fun quickly turns into a series of humorous and troublesome incidents as the dog’s antics lead the duo into various unexpected situations. The film cleverly blends lighthearted humor with themes of friendship, responsibility, and emotional bonding. Neymar, the dog, becomes more than just a pet—he turns into a catalyst for change in the lives of the characters. The movie explores how the presence of an animal can bring people together, heal wounds, and spark growth. With its quirky narrative, energetic performances, and feel-good moments, Neymar appeals to audiences of all ages. The emotional undercurrent adds depth without taking away from the entertainment. It’s a heartwarming tale that highlights how even the most unlikely companions can change lives.
