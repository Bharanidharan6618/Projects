SPOTSTAR - VIKRAM

Vikram is a Tamil action thriller directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj, marking a powerful return for Kamal Haasan. The film opens with a series of mysterious killings carried out by masked vigilantes targeting law enforcement officials. Fahadh Faasil plays Amar, the head of a black‑ops squad tasked with unraveling these deaths, while Vijay Sethupathi portrays Sandhanam, a ruthless drug lord desperate to recover missing drug shipments. Haasan’s Karnan—later revealed to be Vikram—emerges as a former agent seeking justice and orchestrating a covert crusade against the syndicate. The narrative layers deception and hidden identities, keeping the viewer guessing well into the interval. After the mid‑point revelation, the pace shifts into relentless action, with explosive set pieces and high‑stakes confrontations dominating the climax. The trio of performances—Haasan, Sethupathi, and Faasil—are compelling and carry emotional weight. The soundtrack by Anirudh Ravichander and tight editing amplify the adrenaline while maintaining focus on character arcs. Cinematography and choreography deliver gritty realism and cinematic flair in equal measure. Though some critics note that the final act favors spectacle over story, the buildup remains taut and engaging. Vikram blends espionage, drama, and vigilante justice into a layered experience. It also expands a broader cinematic universe, linking back to earlier films in the director’s interconnected saga. In essence, the movie stands out as a visually striking and narratively ambitious thriller grounded in strong performances and meticulous world‑building.
