SPOTSTAR - 96

96 is a deeply nostalgic Tamil romantic drama directed by C. Prem Kumar. It follows Ram, a travel photographer who returns to his hometown and attends a school reunion 22 years after graduation. There he unexpectedly encounters Jaanu, his childhood sweetheart, setting off a flood of bittersweet memories. Their reconnection unfolds over the course of one long night, as the two share why they drifted apart and what has become of their lives. Trisha’s portrayal of Jaanu complements Vijay Sethupathi’s sensitive performance as Ram, creating a chemistry that feels both authentic and tender. The film is remarkable for conveying unspoken emotions—very little is openly declared, yet the longing is palpable. The music by Govind Vasantha beautifully underlines the emotional undercurrents with soulful and evocative melodies. The cinematography captures both the innocence of youth and the weight of lost opportunities. Some viewers describe it as a slow‑burn romance that moves deliberately, yet impactfully. As one fan put it, “Some movies hit differently at different points of time… most beautiful love story of all time.” Others observe that it may not appeal to fast‑paced watchers, but find its emotional honesty powerful. In essence, 96 is a gentle, haunting exploration of love, regret, memory, and the enduring pull of first love.
