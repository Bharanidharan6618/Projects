SPOTSTAR - HEY RAM

Hey Ram is a bold and emotionally charged Tamil-Hindi bilingual film written, directed, and acted by Kamal Haasan. The story unfolds during one of the most turbulent periods in Indian history—the Partition—and follows the emotional and ideological transformation of Saket Ram, an archaeologist whose life is shattered by personal tragedy. After his wife is brutally killed during communal riots, Saket is consumed by grief and hatred, leading him down a dangerous path of revenge and extremism. He becomes part of a conspiracy to assassinate Mahatma Gandhi, believing that violence is the only solution. However, as he journeys through pain and anger, he gradually begins to question his beliefs and understand the deeper meaning of peace and humanity. Kamal Haasan’s performance is intense and layered, capturing the psychological and emotional turmoil of a man torn between vengeance and conscience. The film explores themes of communalism, forgiveness, identity, and transformation with powerful imagery and gripping storytelling. With strong historical references and philosophical undertones, Hey Ram challenges viewers to reflect on the true cost of hatred. It remains one of Indian cinema's most daring and thought-provoking films, blending personal pain with national history.
