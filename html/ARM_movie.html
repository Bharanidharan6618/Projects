SPOTSTAR - ARM

ARM is a Malayalam fantasy action-adventure drama directed by Jithin Lal in his debut feature. The story spans three distinct eras—1900, 1950, and 1990—where each period highlights a different incarnation of the hero, all portrayed by Tovino Thomas. In each timeline, the character—be it Maniyan, Kunjikelu, or Ajayan—is on a mission to protect a sacred treasure tied to their land’s heritage. The narrative blends folklore, myth, and grand spectacle to weave a tale of legacy and justice. Tovino Thomas delivers a standout performance, shaping each version of the hero with subtle nuance and emotional weight. The film is visually breathtaking, set against lush landscapes and rich period detail that bring each era to life. Its adventurous spirit is grounded by thematic depth, exploring heroism, sacrifice, and the fight to preserve cultural identity. The screenplay holds together multiple timelines with careful pacing, although it occasionally stumbles in smooth transitions. Supporting performances deliver strength, especially in emotionally charged moments. The music and sound design elevate the cinematic scope, creating a sense of wonder and urgency. ARM is celebrated for its ambition and scale—rare in regional fantasy films—and emerges as a landmark in Tovino Thomas’s career.
