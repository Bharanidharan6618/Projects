SPOTSTAR - JURASSIC PARK

Jurassic Park is a thrilling science fiction adventure that follows a group of scientists and visitors invited to a remote island where a groundbreaking theme park has been created. The park’s main attraction is living dinosaurs, brought back to life through advanced genetic engineering. Visionary billionaire John Hammond believes his creation will amaze the world, but things take a dangerous turn when security systems fail. Predatory dinosaurs, including the fearsome Tyrannosaurus rex and the cunning velociraptors, escape their enclosures, putting everyone’s lives at risk. Paleontologist Dr. Alan Grant, paleobotanist Dr. Ellie Sattler, and mathematician Dr. Ian Malcolm must use their skills and wits to survive. The group battles against time and nature as they try to escape the island alive. Along the way, the story explores themes of human ambition, the unpredictability of nature, and the consequences of playing with science beyond our control. With intense action, moments of wonder, and gripping suspense, Jurassic Park remains a powerful reminder that life finds a way.
