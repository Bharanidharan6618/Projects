SPOTSTAR - SARDAR

Sardar is a gripping Tamil action thriller that explores themes of espionage, betrayal, and redemption across two generations. The story revolves around a sincere police officer, Vijay Prakash, who uncovers a dangerous conspiracy involving national security and corporate greed. As he digs deeper, he discovers shocking truths about his own family—particularly his estranged father, Sardar, a legendary spy who has been in hiding for decades. Karthi plays dual roles as both the suave cop and the aged, intelligent spy, delivering a powerful and versatile performance. The film moves between past and present, revealing how Sardar was betrayed by the very system he served and how he now returns to protect the country from a looming threat. The emotional conflict between father and son adds depth to the action-packed narrative. The film is fast-paced, with well-choreographed stunts and a tight screenplay that keeps the tension alive. The supporting characters are well-etched, and the emotional beats are handled with care. The soundtrack and background score heighten the suspense and emotional weight. Sardar is not just about heroism but also about choices, sacrifice, and the true meaning of patriotism. It delivers both thrill and heart, making it a memorable cinematic experience.
