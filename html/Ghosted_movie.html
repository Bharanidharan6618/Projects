SPOTSTAR - GHOSTED

Ghosted is a romantic action-comedy that follows the story of Cole, a charming yet ordinary man, and Sadie, a mysterious woman he meets by chance. Their connection is instant, leading Cole to believe he has found someone truly special. However, after Sadie suddenly stops responding to his messages, he decides to surprise her by traveling across the world to find her. To his shock, Cole discovers that Sadie is actually a highly skilled secret agent caught up in a dangerous mission. What begins as a romantic gesture quickly turns into a wild adventure filled with chases, explosions, and unexpected twists. Cole is reluctantly pulled into Sadie’s high-stakes world, forcing him to face danger he never imagined. Their journey is a blend of awkward romance, witty banter, and adrenaline-pumping action. As they navigate assassins, betrayals, and global threats, their chemistry deepens in the most unpredictable circumstances. Ghosted delivers a mix of humor, heart, and high-energy sequences, making it both thrilling and lighthearted. It explores how love can spark in the unlikeliest of situations and how trust is built under extreme pressure. The film balances romance and action, keeping viewers entertained until the very end.
