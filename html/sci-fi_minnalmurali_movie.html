SPOTSTAR - MINNAL MURALI

Minnal Murali is a unique superhero film set in a small Kerala village that brings a fresh, grounded take to the genre. The story follows Jaison, an ordinary tailor with big dreams, who unexpectedly gains superpowers after being struck by lightning. As he discovers his abilities, he adopts the heroic persona of Minnal Murali and begins to defend his community with humor and heart. At the same time, another lightning strike transforms Shibu, a socially ostracized man, into a complex antagonist battling his own demons. The film stands out for giving the villain a tragic, emotionally compelling backstory, resulting in a nuanced clash between hero and antihero. Jaison’s innocence and evolving sense of responsibility make him instantly relatable, while Shibu’s descent into darkness highlights how society shapes individuals. The director’s storytelling anchors the supernatural in real-world feelings and relationships, enriched by nostalgic rural charm. The film’s grounded approach is further elevated through clever writing and strong performances, especially by the leads. Fight scenes have playful creativity, and visual flair is balanced by emotional depth. Minnal Murali succeeds as both a celebration of homegrown heroism and a heartfelt reflection on belonging, power, and empathy.
