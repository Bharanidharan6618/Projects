SPOTSTAR - IRUTTU

Iruttu is a Tamil supernatural thriller that stands out for its intense atmosphere and unique storyline. The film revolves around a police officer named Chezhiyan who is transferred to a hill station with his family. Soon after arriving, he is drawn into a series of mysterious and unnatural deaths that leave no clues. As he investigates, he realizes that the threat is not human but something far more sinister. The film combines elements of horror and suspense with a strong investigative angle. Sundar C plays the lead role with seriousness and conviction, anchoring the narrative with a grounded performance. The eerie visuals and sound design create a chilling atmosphere throughout the film. Unlike typical horror films, Iruttu explores ancient legends and supernatural forces rooted in dark mythology. The plot moves with tension and builds up to an intense climax. The screenplay is sharp, with minimal comedy and distractions, keeping the focus on horror and suspense. The film avoids clichés and delivers a fresh take on the genre. Iruttu is a gripping experience for viewers who enjoy horror with depth and mystery. It leaves a lasting impression with its dark tone and intelligent storytelling.
