Nine is a Malayalam science fiction psychological thriller that blends elements of mystery, drama, and the supernatural. The story revolves around Dr. Louis, an astrophysicist and single father, who lives with his young son, Adam. Their lives take an unexpected turn when a rare astronomical event causes the Earth to experience nine days of darkness. During this period, Louis takes Adam to a remote hill station, hoping to study the phenomenon while bonding with his son. However, their isolation is disturbed by the arrival of a mysterious woman whose presence begins to unsettle Louis. Strange events unfold, blurring the lines between reality and hallucination. Louis finds himself haunted by his traumatic past and forced to confront his inner fears. The relationship between father and son becomes central as danger grows, both from external and psychological sources. The film skillfully intertwines science with human emotion, creating an atmosphere of tension and wonder. With its breathtaking visuals and haunting background score, Nine keeps the audience questioning what is real and what exists only in the mind. It is a story about loss, love, and the fragile boundaries of human perception.
