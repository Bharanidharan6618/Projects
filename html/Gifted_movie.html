SPOTSTAR - GIFTED

Gifted is a heartfelt drama that tells the story of Frank Adler, a single man raising his young niece, Mary, in a small coastal town in Florida. Mary is no ordinary child—she is a math prodigy with exceptional intellectual abilities. Frank, however, wants her to have a normal childhood, away from the pressures and isolation that can come with such talent. Their quiet life is disrupted when Mary’s grandmother, Evelyn, learns of her abilities and begins a legal battle for custody, believing Mary should be pushed to achieve greatness. The conflict forces Frank to confront painful truths about his late sister, Mary’s mother, who also struggled under the weight of exceptional expectations. As the custody case unfolds, Frank must balance protecting Mary’s happiness with honoring her potential. The bond between uncle and niece is tested but also deepened through love, trust, and sacrifice. Chris Evans delivers a touching performance as Frank, showing both strength and vulnerability. McKenna Grace shines as Mary, capturing the wit, brilliance, and emotional depth of her character. The film blends moments of humor with emotional drama, leaving a lasting message about family, choice, and what truly defines success.
