SPOTSTAR - PANCHATHANDIRAM

Panchathanthiram is a Tamil comedy film starring Kamal Haasan in the lead role and directed by K. S. Ravikumar. The movie follows Ram, a successful pilot who gets caught up in a hilarious mess because of a misunderstanding. Though he's a loyal husband, his friends think he needs cheering up after a fight with his wife. They secretly arrange a night out with a call girl to lift his mood, but things spiral out of control when the girl is found dead. What follows is a series of chaotic events as Ram and his four close friends try to hide the situation. Each attempt to fix the mess leads to more confusion and laughter. Kamal Haasan shines with his perfect comic timing, while the ensemble cast adds strong support. The dialogues, penned by Crazy Mohan, are fast-paced, witty, and full of clever wordplay. The film combines friendship, mistaken identity, and dark humor into a tightly packed story. Despite the seemingly wild plot, the narrative is smooth and engaging. Panchathanthiram is considered a cult classic in Tamil cinema for its humor and writing. The chemistry among the actors makes it even more enjoyable. It remains one of the most loved comedy films in Tamil for all the right reasons.
