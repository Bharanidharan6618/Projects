SPOTSTAR - PIZZA

Pizza is a gripping Tamil horror-thriller that keeps the audience on edge with its unexpected twists and chilling atmosphere. The story revolves around Michael, a pizza delivery boy with a simple life and a loving girlfriend, Anu. Things take a terrifying turn when he goes to deliver a pizza to a house, only to find himself trapped in a nightmarish sequence of supernatural events. The eerie setting, sudden scares, and suspenseful plot build a sense of dread that intensifies with every scene. Vijay Sethupathi delivers a standout performance, perfectly capturing Michael's fear and confusion. The film, directed by Karthik Subbaraj, cleverly blends horror with psychological suspense, keeping viewers guessing about what’s real and what’s not. The cinematography and sound design add to the haunting feel of the movie. As the story unfolds, it challenges the audience’s expectations and reveals a shocking twist that changes everything. Pizza is not just a horror film but a smartly crafted narrative filled with clever storytelling. It marked a turning point in Tamil cinema for low-budget, high-quality thrillers. The film’s originality, strong performances, and gripping screenplay make it a memorable watch.
