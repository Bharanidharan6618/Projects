SPOTSTAR - ANDROID KUNJAPPAN Ver 5.25

Android Kunjappan Version 5.25 is a heartwarming Malayalam drama that beautifully blends science fiction with deep human emotions. The story follows Bhaskaran, an aging, stubborn father living in a small village, whose son Subrahmanian works abroad as a mechanical engineer. Unable to care for his father personally, Subrahmanian sends home a humanoid robot caregiver named Kunjappan. Initially, Bhaskaran is hostile toward the machine, seeing it as an intruder in his simple, traditional life. However, over time, an unexpected bond forms between the old man and the robot, leading to moments of humor, tenderness, and bittersweet reflection. The film explores themes of loneliness, companionship, generational differences, and the intersection of technology with human life. It raises questions about what truly defines care and love in an increasingly modernized world. With its mix of satire, emotional depth, and rural charm, the narrative keeps viewers engaged from start to finish. The evolving relationship between Bhaskaran and Kunjappan forms the emotional core of the story. Ultimately, it leaves the audience reflecting on family, aging, and the irreplaceable warmth of human connection.
