SPOTSTAR - BHOOTHAKAALAM

Bhoothakaalam is a Malayalam psychological horror drama that masterfully combines emotional depth with chilling suspense. The story revolves around a widowed mother and her grown-up son who live in a modest house in Thiruvananthapuram. Their relationship is strained due to unresolved tensions and the son’s struggle with unemployment and depression. As they try to navigate their emotional turmoil, strange and unsettling events begin to occur in their home. The line between psychological distress and supernatural presence becomes increasingly blurred. The house itself starts to feel like a living, breathing entity filled with an ominous presence. Revathy delivers a remarkable performance as the troubled mother, portraying both vulnerability and quiet strength. Shane Nigam’s nuanced acting captures the frustration and helplessness of a young man battling personal demons. The film relies more on atmosphere, sound design, and tension than on jump scares, making the horror feel grounded and real. Themes of grief, mental health, and fear of the unknown run deep throughout the narrative. The pacing is deliberate, building towards an intense and unsettling climax. Bhoothakaalam leaves viewers with lingering questions, making it as thought-provoking as it is terrifying.
