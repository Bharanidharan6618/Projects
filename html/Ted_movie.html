SPOTSTAR - TED

Ted is a raunchy comedy about John Bennett, a man whose childhood wish brings his teddy bear to life. As a boy, John is overjoyed when Ted magically becomes his real best friend, and the two grow up together sharing laughs, adventures, and plenty of mischief. Decades later, Ted is still alive, but he’s now a foul-mouthed, party-loving bear with no intention of growing up. John struggles to balance his relationship with his long-time girlfriend, Lori, and his loyalty to Ted, who constantly gets him into trouble. The story mixes outrageous humor with moments of genuine friendship, showing how hard it can be to let go of the past. As John faces the choice between responsibility and clinging to his childhood, Ted proves that he’s more than just a stuffed toy — he’s family. The film blends crude jokes, absurd situations, and heartfelt themes, making it a unique mix of inappropriate hilarity and unexpected warmth.
