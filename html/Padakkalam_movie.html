SPOTSTAR - PADAKKALAM

Padakkalam is a quirky Malayalam fantasy comedy set in a vibrant college campus where four nerdy engineering students—Jithin, Ramsad, Nakul, and Kannan—lead ordinary lives until a supernatural twist flips everything. A charismatic professor, Renjith, uses an enchanted Pachisi game to magically control his rival, the head of the department, creating chaos in the college. What begins as innocent curiosity turns into a thrilling and humorous battle of wits as the students uncover the dark secret behind the artifact and attempt to undo the spell. The film smoothly blends light-hearted campus hijinks with elements of fantasy, magic, and mischief. Suraj Venjaramoodu and Sharaf U Dheen bring charm and eccentricity to their roles, while Sandeep Pradeep shines as the emotional core amid the madness. The narrative is playful yet fast-paced, filled with twists and amusing spectacles. The faculty-student dynamics fuel the comedic tension and heighten the stakes. Padakkalam stands out with its imaginative premise, engaging dialogue, and youthful energy. It's a refreshing campus entertainer where magic meets humor, offering a distinct flavor in contemporary Malayalam cinema.
