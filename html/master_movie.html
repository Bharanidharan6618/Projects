SPOTSTAR - MASTER

Master is a Tamil action drama directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj, featuring Vijay and Vijay Sethupathi in powerful lead roles. The story revolves around JD, a carefree college professor with a drinking habit, who is sent to a juvenile correctional facility to reform the students. There, he crosses paths with Bhavani, a ruthless gangster who uses the facility to recruit young criminals for his empire. As JD begins to understand the deep-rooted corruption, he takes it upon himself to challenge Bhavani’s control and bring justice. Vijay brings charm, energy, and subtle emotion to his role, delivering a mix of humor and intensity. Vijay Sethupathi stands out as a calm but terrifying villain, creating a gripping contrast. The film’s music, composed by Anirudh Ravichander, adds immense energy and emotion to the scenes. Action sequences are grounded and impactful, reflecting both physical struggle and emotional stakes. The movie balances mass appeal with meaningful moments, especially in scenes involving the young inmates. Cinematography and editing keep the visual style sleek and engaging. Though the second half slows down slightly, the film maintains its momentum through strong performances. Master ultimately speaks about responsibility, influence, and redemption. It offers a compelling mix of entertainment and thought, making it memorable for a wide audience.
