SPOTSTAR - HRIDAYAM

Hridayam is a heartfelt coming-of-age drama that follows the emotional and personal journey of Arun Neelakandan, from his carefree college days to the responsibilities of adulthood. Directed by Vineeth Sreenivasan and starring Pranav Mohanlal in the lead, the film captures the highs and lows of youth, love, heartbreak, friendship, and self-discovery. Arun’s life begins in an engineering college in Chennai, where he experiences intense friendships, a passionate romance, and painful betrayals that shape his emotional growth. As time moves forward, the story showcases how Arun matures, learns from his mistakes, and rebuilds himself both personally and professionally. The film’s music, composed by Hesham Abdul Wahab, plays a vital role in enhancing its emotional depth and resonance. Hridayam beautifully reflects on how people and priorities change over time, and how every phase of life leaves a mark on one's heart. The visuals are vibrant and nostalgic, effectively bringing college life and young adulthood to the screen. Supporting characters play meaningful roles, adding richness to the narrative. The film’s realistic storytelling and emotional relatability make it a touching experience for audiences of all ages. Hridayam stands out as a sincere and soul-stirring portrayal of life’s journey.
