SPOTSTAR - RAAVANAN

Raavanan is a gripping Tamil action drama directed by Mani Ratnam, inspired by the epic Ramayana but told from a fresh perspective. The story centers around Veeraiya, a feared tribal leader who kidnaps Ragini, the wife of a high-ranking police officer, as revenge for the injustice done to his people. As the story unfolds, Ragini begins to see the human side of Veeraiya and questions the true meaning of good and evil. Vikram delivers a powerful and intense performance as Veeraiya, capturing the complexity of a man shaped by pain and rebellion. Aishwarya Rai portrays Ragini with strength and grace, adding emotional depth to the story. Prithviraj plays the police officer, embodying righteousness and duty, yet also raising moral questions. The film is rich in visuals, set against stunning natural backdrops that enhance its emotional tone. A. R. Rahman’s music adds soul to the narrative, perfectly blending with the intense storytelling. Raavanan challenges traditional views of right and wrong, portraying its characters in shades of grey. The emotional tension between love, justice, and revenge runs deep throughout the film. With layered characters and poetic visuals, Raavanan stands as a bold retelling of an age-old tale through a modern lens.
