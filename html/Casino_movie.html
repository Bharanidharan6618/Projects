SPOTSTAR - CASINO

Casino is a gripping crime drama that delves into the high-stakes world of Las Vegas during the 1970s. The story follows Sam “Ace” Rothstein, a meticulous and skilled casino manager chosen by the mob to run the Tangiers Casino. Ace turns the place into a profitable empire through discipline, calculation, and an eye for detail. His life becomes complicated when he marries Ginger, a glamorous but troubled woman whose loyalty is always uncertain. Meanwhile, Ace’s childhood friend Nicky Santoro arrives in Vegas, bringing with him a violent and reckless approach to business that begins to attract unwanted attention. As greed, jealousy, and power struggles grow, tensions escalate both inside and outside the casino. The relationships between Ace, Ginger, and Nicky spiral into chaos, fueled by mistrust and betrayal. The film explores the corruption, glamour, and brutality that lie beneath Vegas’s glittering façade. Power proves intoxicating but dangerous, and the higher they climb, the harder they fall. In the end, the empire they built begins to crumble under the weight of lies, greed, and inevitable consequences.
