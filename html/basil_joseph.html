SPOTSTAR - BASIL JOSEPH

BASIL JOSEPH

Basil Joseph is a prominent Indian filmmaker and actor known for his work in the Malayalam film industry. Born on April 28, 1989, in Sultan Bathery, Wayanad, Kerala, he initially pursued a career in engineering and worked at Infosys before transitioning to cinema. His passion for storytelling and humor led him to the world of filmmaking, where he started by assisting directors and later made his debut as a director with the film Kunjiramayanam in 2015. He gained wider recognition with Godha, a sports drama, and achieved massive success with the superhero film Minnal Murali, which became a landmark in Malayalam cinema. Apart from directing, Basil has also made a mark as a talented actor, known for his natural comic timing and emotional depth. His performances in films like Jaya Jaya Jaya Jaya Hey and Palthu Janwar were well-received by both audiences and critics. In recent years, he has balanced directing and acting roles with ease, showing versatility and consistent growth. Basil is admired for his humble personality, creative vision, and ability to connect with viewers through relatable storytelling. He continues to be a significant force in contemporary Malayalam cinema.
