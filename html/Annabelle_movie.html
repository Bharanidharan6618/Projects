SPOTSTAR - ANNABELLE

Annabelle is a supernatural horror film centered on a possessed vintage doll that becomes the conduit for a dark presence. The story begins with John and Mia, a young married couple expecting their first child, who acquire the doll as part of a collection. Their peaceful life shatters when their home is invaded by members of a satanic cult, leaving behind a terrifying mark that summons a malevolent entity. Soon after, disturbing events begin—objects move on their own, strange noises echo in the night, and the doll seems to appear in different places without explanation. Mia, recovering from the attack and caring for her newborn, becomes increasingly convinced that the doll is linked to the haunting. As the supernatural occurrences grow more violent, they seek help from a priest, who reveals the entity’s true intention—to claim a human soul. The tension escalates as the demon targets their baby, leading to a desperate struggle to protect her. The doll’s eerie stare and unchanging smile become a constant, menacing presence in their home. Ultimately, the evil proves difficult to destroy, and Annabelle ends up in the possession of others, suggesting the curse is far from over. The film blends jump scares, creeping dread, and occult lore to create a chilling prelude to The Conjuring universe.
