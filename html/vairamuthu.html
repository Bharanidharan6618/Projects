SPOTSTAR - VAIRAMUTHU

VAIRAMUTHU

Vairamuthu is one of the most celebrated and influential lyricists in Tamil cinema, known for his poetic depth, philosophical reflections, and rich use of the Tamil language. Born on July 13, 1953, in a village called Mettur in Tamil Nadu, he developed a passion for literature and poetry from a young age. He began his film career in the early 1980s and made a powerful debut with the film Nizhalgal. His collaboration with composer Ilaiyaraaja produced several unforgettable songs that are still cherished by music lovers. Later, his partnership with A.R. Rahman in films like Roja, Bombay, and Alaipayuthey brought a new dimension to Tamil film lyrics. Vairamuthu’s writing is known for its elegance, literary quality, and ability to convey deep emotions in simple words. He often draws inspiration from nature, love, rural life, and social issues, making his songs relatable and meaningful. Over the years, he has written thousands of songs and earned numerous awards, including multiple National Film Awards. His contributions to Tamil literature extend beyond cinema, as he has authored several novels and poetry collections. Vairamuthu has also been recognized with the Padma Shri and Padma Bhushan for his literary achievements. His words have the power to evoke powerful imagery and stir the soul of the listener. Even after decades in the industry, he continues to influence generations of lyricists and poets with his timeless style.
