SPOTSTAR - HOME

Home is a heartwarming Malayalam film that beautifully explores the emotional gap between generations within a family. The story revolves around Oliver Twist, a kind and simple middle-aged man who struggles to connect with his tech-savvy sons. Despite his genuine efforts, he often finds himself out of place in the fast-paced, digital world his children live in. As his eldest son, an aspiring filmmaker, returns home to work on a script, tensions begin to rise between personal space and familial love. The film gently delves into themes of loneliness, relevance, and the silent sacrifices of parents. Oliver’s character, played with touching sincerity, becomes the emotional anchor of the story. The narrative is filled with moments of subtle humor, nostalgia, and emotional depth. Every character is relatable, and the setting feels incredibly authentic. The film doesn’t rely on melodrama but rather draws strength from its realism. As misunderstandings unfold, so does the realization of unconditional love within families. Home reminds us of the importance of empathy, time, and human connection. It's a poignant tribute to the unsung heroes of every household – our parents.
