SPOTSTAR - RDX

RDX is a high-octane Malayalam action drama that revolves around the intense bond between three friends — Robert, Dony, and Xavier — whose initials give the film its title. Set against the backdrop of the 1990s, the movie explores themes of friendship, loyalty, and revenge. The trio, known for their martial arts prowess, get entangled in a violent chain of events that disrupts their peaceful lives. What starts as a youthful rivalry soon spirals into a deep conflict that tests their unity and strength. The film shifts between past and present, revealing the consequences of their choices and the emotional toll it takes on their families. The action sequences are gripping and choreographed with a raw intensity that adds to the film’s adrenaline. Each character is given depth, showing not just their physical strength but also their emotional vulnerabilities. The brotherhood among the leads forms the emotional core, elevating the film beyond just a revenge drama. With a fast-paced narrative and a strong emotional undercurrent, RDX delivers both thrills and heart. The story is a blend of nostalgia, grit, and redemption.
