SPOTSTAR - THE CONJURING 3

The Conjuring 3, also known as The Conjuring: The Devil Made Me Do It, follows paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren as they take on one of their most chilling and unusual cases. The story begins with a harrowing exorcism of a young boy, during which a demonic presence transfers itself to a young man named Arne Johnson. Soon after, Arne commits a brutal murder, claiming that he was possessed by the devil at the time. The Warrens investigate, determined to prove his innocence in court using supernatural evidence. Their search uncovers a dark trail leading to a Satanic curse connected to multiple deaths. Lorraine’s psychic visions grow increasingly disturbing, drawing her deeper into the mind of the occultist behind the curse. Ed, meanwhile, struggles with health issues but remains steadfast in protecting his wife and solving the case. The film blends courtroom drama with intense supernatural horror, creating a unique mix of mystery and terror. It delves into themes of faith, love, and the dangers of tampering with dark forces. The suspense builds as the Warrens race against time to break the curse and save Arne’s soul. In the end, it reinforces their enduring bond and dedication to fighting evil no matter the personal cost.
