SPOTSTAR - JOJI

Joji is a dark Malayalam crime drama inspired by Shakespeare’s Macbeth, set in the backdrop of a wealthy family living in rural Kerala. The story follows Joji, the youngest son of a strict and dominating patriarch, who is seen as a lazy and aimless man by his family. When his father suffers a sudden health setback, Joji begins to see an opportunity to seize control and live life on his own terms. His quiet and cunning nature hides deep ambition and greed, pushing him to make morally corrupt decisions. The movie unfolds in a slow-burn style, showing how ambition and selfishness can spiral into crime and tragedy. Fahadh Faasil’s portrayal of Joji adds layers of subtlety, making the character both intriguing and unsettling. The film’s minimalistic storytelling, natural performances, and rural setting create an eerie, realistic atmosphere. It also delves into family dynamics, unspoken tensions, and how power struggles can destroy relationships. With its tense narrative and psychological depth, Joji leaves a haunting impression on the audience.
