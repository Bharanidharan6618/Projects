SPOTSTAR - FIGHT CLUB

Fight Club is a psychological drama that explores identity, consumerism, and rebellion through a dark and twisted narrative. The story follows an unnamed narrator, a disillusioned office worker suffering from chronic insomnia, who finds temporary relief by attending support groups for illnesses he doesn’t have. His life changes when he meets Tyler Durden, a charismatic soap salesman with a radical worldview. Together, they form Fight Club, an underground group where men release their frustrations through bare-knuckle brawls. What starts as a secret outlet for anger evolves into something more dangerous as Tyler transforms the club into Project Mayhem, a movement aimed at dismantling societal structures. The narrator becomes increasingly unsettled by Tyler’s extremism and the chaos that follows. As tensions rise, he makes a shocking discovery about Tyler’s true identity, forcing him to confront his fractured sense of self. The film delves into themes of masculinity, control, and the emptiness of materialistic lifestyles. Its gritty visuals and sharp dialogue create an unsettling yet thought-provoking atmosphere. By the end, the narrator must make a drastic choice to reclaim his life from the destructive path he helped create.
