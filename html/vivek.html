SPOTSTAR - VIVEK

VIVEK

Vivek is a prominent Indian lyricist known for his impactful and thoughtful lyrics in Tamil cinema. He began his career in the early 2010s and quickly rose to fame with his meaningful writing style that blends emotion, social relevance, and modern sensibility. Vivek has penned lyrics for many chart-topping songs across various genres, from romantic melodies to motivational anthems. He gained wide recognition for his work in films like Kaththi, Nanban, and Bigil, where his words struck a chord with audiences of all ages. One of his biggest breakthroughs came with the film Sarkar, where his lyrics reflected powerful political and social themes. He is also known for collaborating with top music directors like A.R. Rahman, Anirudh Ravichander, and D. Imman. Vivek's writing often carries layers of meaning, making listeners reflect deeply while enjoying the rhythm and melody. His ability to adapt to changing musical trends while maintaining poetic quality sets him apart in the industry. He has received numerous accolades for his lyrical contributions and continues to be one of the most sought-after lyricists in Tamil cinema. Apart from film songs, he has also worked on independent music and theme songs that address societal issues. Vivek's passion for Tamil language and literature shines through in every line he writes. His words not only entertain but also inspire, earning him respect from fans and critics alike.
