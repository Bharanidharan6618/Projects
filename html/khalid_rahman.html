SPOTSTAR - KHALID RAHMAN

KHALID RAHMAN

Khalid Rahman is a prominent Malayalam film director and screenwriter known for his refreshing storytelling and diverse filmmaking style. Born on November 15, 1990, in Kochi, Kerala, he comes from a family deeply rooted in cinema, with his father V. P. Khalid being a noted actor and his brothers Shyju and Jimshi Khalid working as cinematographers. He began his film journey as an assistant director on several popular films, gradually building his skills and understanding of the craft. Khalid made his directorial debut with the film Anuraga Karikkin Vellam in 2016, which was well received for its warmth and simplicity. He followed it up with the critically acclaimed film Unda in 2019, starring Mammootty, which dealt with social and political themes in a realistic manner. In 2020, he directed Love, a psychological drama filmed during the pandemic lockdown, showcasing his ability to work under constraints. In 2022, he delivered one of his most successful films, Thallumaala, a high-energy action comedy that became a massive hit among the youth. His recent film Alappuzha Gymkhana, released in 2025, was a sports-based coming-of-age drama that became a sleeper hit. Khalid is known for exploring different genres, never repeating himself, and constantly reinventing his approach to storytelling. His works reflect a mix of grounded narratives, experimental techniques, and strong emotional core. He is also recognized for writing or co-writing many of his films, bringing a personal voice to his projects. Khalid continues to be a key figure in shaping modern Malayalam cinema with his bold choices and relatable stories.
