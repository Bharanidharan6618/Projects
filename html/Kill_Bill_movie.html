SPOTSTAR - KILL BILL:VOL.1

Kill Bill is a stylish action revenge saga that follows the journey of a deadly assassin known as The Bride. Once a member of the elite Deadly Viper Assassination Squad, she is betrayed by her own team, led by her former lover and boss, Bill. Left for dead after a brutal attack on her wedding day, The Bride awakens from a coma four years later with one mission—hunt down and kill every person responsible. Her quest takes her across continents, facing assassins with unique skills, from the sword-wielding O-Ren Ishii to the relentless Vernita Green. Along the way, she trains under the legendary martial arts master Pai Mei to perfect her combat abilities. The battles are fierce, blending samurai sword fights, martial arts, and intense gunplay. Each confrontation is more personal and violent than the last, as The Bride slowly works her way toward Bill. The film is told in chapters, mixing non-linear storytelling with vivid visuals and stylized action. At its heart, it is a tale of vengeance, resilience, and the cost of a life built on violence. By the time she reaches Bill, it is not just a fight for revenge, but a reckoning with her past and her future.
