SPOTSTAR - ZODIAC

Zodiac is a gripping crime thriller based on the real-life hunt for the elusive Zodiac Killer, who terrorized California during the late 1960s and early 1970s. The film follows the intertwined lives of a political cartoonist, Robert Graysmith, a crime reporter, Paul Avery, and two detectives, Dave Toschi and Bill Armstrong, as they become obsessed with solving the case. The killer taunts authorities and the public by sending cryptic letters and ciphers to newspapers, some of which are never fully decoded. As the investigation drags on, the lack of concrete evidence and the killer’s ability to vanish without a trace frustrates everyone involved. The obsession begins to consume their personal lives, straining relationships and careers. Graysmith, in particular, dedicates years to piecing together clues long after official interest has faded. The film captures the painstaking, often tedious nature of real investigations, showing how leads can turn cold and suspects slip away. Tension builds as the mystery deepens, but answers remain just out of reach. The story avoids offering a definitive solution, reflecting the real case’s enduring uncertainty. Zodiac blends suspense with meticulous detail, creating a haunting portrayal of obsession and the cost of seeking the truth.
