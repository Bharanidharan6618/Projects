A.R.RAHMAN

A.R. Rahman is one of the most iconic and respected music composers in the world, known for his soul-stirring melodies and groundbreaking fusion of Indian and Western music. Born on January 6, 1967, in Chennai as A.S. Dileep Kumar, he converted to Islam and took the name Allah Rakha Rahman. He began his career composing jingles for advertisements before making a stunning debut with the film Roja in 1992, which earned him national fame. Rahman’s unique sound, combining classical Indian instruments with electronic music and global influences, brought a new dimension to Indian film music. He has composed for numerous hit films in multiple languages, including Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, and English. Some of his most celebrated works include soundtracks for Bombay, Dil Se, Lagaan, Swades, Rockstar, and OK Kanmani. His international success peaked with the Oscar-winning score for the film Slumdog Millionaire, where he won two Academy Awards. Rahman is also a skilled singer, music producer, and philanthropist, and he runs his own music school in Chennai. His contributions to music have earned him the title “Mozart of Madras” and numerous national and international honors. He is known for his spiritual nature, humility, and constant innovation. Rahman's music continues to inspire millions across the world and has left an everlasting mark on the global music scene. His ability to blend emotion with technology makes him a timeless musical genius.
