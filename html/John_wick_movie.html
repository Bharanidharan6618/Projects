SPOTSTAR - JOHN WICK-3

John Wick 3, also known as John Wick: Chapter 3 – Parabellum, picks up immediately after the events of the second film, with John on the run after breaking the High Table’s sacred rule by killing on Continental grounds. Declared “excommunicado,” he loses all protection and has a $14 million bounty placed on his head, drawing every assassin in New York into the hunt. Injured and desperate, John navigates through relentless ambushes, using his unmatched combat skills and resourcefulness to survive. He seeks help from old allies, including The Director and Sofia, who assist him despite the risks. The action intensifies with breathtaking fight sequences, from knife battles to motorcycle chases and brutal close-quarter combat. John’s journey takes him from the neon-lit streets of New York to the deserts of Morocco, as he seeks the Elder—the only person who can lift his bounty. But even with this chance, alliances are fragile, and betrayal looms. Meanwhile, Winston and the Continental face their own power struggle with the High Table. The film combines stylish choreography, high stakes, and the unyielding determination of a man who refuses to die easily. It ends with John betrayed once again, barely surviving, and ready to wage war against the system that turned on him.
