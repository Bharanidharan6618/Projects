SPOTSTAR - MALIK

Malik is a Malayalam political thriller directed by Mahesh Narayanan, featuring a commanding performance by Fahadh Faasil as Sulaiman. The film opens with a stunning single‑take sequence that introduces Sulaiman’s home and community in a coastal township, establishing his role as a respected yet feared leader. Told in a non‑linear style, the narrative traces his rise from youthful smuggler to a powerful protector of his people. His journey is shaped by friendship, betrayal, communal tensions, and corrupt politics. The film delivers a compelling portrait of how systems forge both heroes and villains through manipulation and violence. Nimisha Sajayan shines as Roselyn, Sulaiman’s partner, whose quiet strength anchors the emotional arc of the story. Editor-director Narayanan’s pacing and storytelling ensure each flashback adds new complexity and depth. Supporting roles by Vinay Forrt, Joju George, and Dileesh Pothan enrich the layered ensemble. The film also explores themes of identity, loyalty, and sacrifice without pulling punches. Cinematography captures the gritty beauty of Kerala’s coastal life, while thoughtful visuals heighten dramatic impact ([lensmenreviews.com][1]). While lengthy at over two and a half hours, Malik earns its runtime through emotional weight and moral complexity. It remains one of the most ambitious and resonant political crime sagas in recent Malayalam cinema.
