SPOTSTAR - 2.0

2.0 is a visually grand Tamil science fiction spectacle that follows the story of Dr. Vaseegaran and his robot creation Chitti as they battle an unprecedented technological threat. The film begins with mobile phones mysteriously coming alive and attacking the public, driven by an unknown force. Dr. Vaseegaran teams up with his loyal robot Chitti to uncover the mastermind behind the chaos, leading to the emergence of a deadly adversary named Pakshi Rajan. Pakshi Rajan is a former ornithologist transformed into a vengeful spirit harnessing birds to fight for ecological justice. The conflict becomes a technological and philosophical clash, exploring themes of nature, technology, and humanity. Rajinikanth returns in dual roles with magnetic presence, bringing both emotional depth and heroic energy. Akshay Kumar plays the powerful antagonist with intensity and conviction. The film blends high-octane action sequences with emotional moments rooted in loss and redemption. Stunning visual effects elevate battles between Chitti and Pakshi Rajan, especially in aerial and robotic showdowns. The narrative raises questions about unchecked technological growth and its impact on the environment. The chemistry between humans and machines is explored with emotional weight. Director Shankar’s signature blend of scale, spectacle, and sentiment is fully on display. 2.0 is an ambitious cinematic experience that pushes the boundaries of Indian mainstream film in scope and imagination.
