SPOTSTAR - DRAGON

"Dragon" is a Tamil film that follows the journey of Ragavan, a once-brilliant student who loses his way during his college years. Nicknamed “Dragon” for his fiery personality, he squanders his potential, failing multiple subjects and drifting into a life without purpose. After his girlfriend Keerthi calls him a failure and leaves him, Ragavan is forced to confront the reality of his choices. Determined to prove himself, he fakes his credentials to land a job, but his lies eventually catch up with him. The film explores themes of self-worth, redemption, and the meaning of true success. Pradeep Ranganathan delivers a powerful performance, portraying a flawed but relatable character. The emotional weight of the story is balanced by moments of humor and warmth, especially in the scenes with his father. The narrative arc highlights how a fall from grace can become a turning point rather than an end. With vibrant cinematography and an engaging soundtrack, the movie captures both youthful energy and emotional depth. It is a story about second chances, learning from failure, and finding courage to face the truth. "Dragon" resonates with anyone who has struggled, stumbled, and strived to rise again.
