SPOTSTAR - USTAD HOTEL

Ustad Hotel is a heartwarming Malayalam film that tells the story of Faizi, a young aspiring chef who has just returned from a culinary course in Europe. His passion for cooking clashes with his strict father's expectations, who wants him to become a successful businessman. After a fallout, Faizi moves to Kozhikode and stays with his grandfather, Kareem, who runs a small but beloved beachside restaurant called Ustad Hotel. Through working at the hotel, Faizi begins to understand the deeper meaning of food, not just as a career but as a way to serve love and kindness. His grandfather, a wise and humble man, teaches him the importance of feeding the soul, not just the stomach. Faizi’s journey is not just about finding his own path, but also about reconnecting with people, values, and purpose. The film is filled with warmth, subtle humor, and powerful emotions. It highlights themes like compassion, cultural identity, and social responsibility. The soulful background of Kozhikode and the mouthwatering portrayal of food add richness to the narrative. Dulquer Salmaan delivers a charming and sincere performance as Faizi. Thilakan, as Kareem, leaves a lasting impact with his dignified and heartfelt role. Ustad Hotel is not just a movie about food, but a moving tale of relationships, dreams, and discovering what truly matters in life.
