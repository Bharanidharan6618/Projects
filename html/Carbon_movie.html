SPOTSTAR - CARBON

Carbon is a Malayalam adventure drama that tells the story of Sibi Sebastian, a restless young man constantly chasing quick money and new opportunities. His life takes a turn when he accepts a job to restore an old royal mansion in a remote forest. While working there, he learns about a centuries-old treasure hidden deep within the wilderness, sparking his curiosity and greed. As Sibi ventures further into the dense and mysterious forest, he encounters unpredictable challenges and meets people whose intentions are unclear. The film slowly shifts from a light-hearted beginning to a tense and thrilling journey of survival and self-discovery. Nature itself becomes a character, testing his determination and spirit at every step. The beauty and danger of the wild are portrayed vividly, immersing the viewer in Sibi’s quest. His pursuit of wealth gradually transforms into an inner exploration of meaning and purpose. Carbon blends elements of mystery, adventure, and human drama, keeping the audience invested in Sibi’s fate. The ending leaves room for interpretation, making the story linger in the mind long after it ends.
