SPOTSTAR - SWEENEY TODD

Sweeney Todd: The Demon Barber of Fleet Street is a dark musical thriller directed by Tim Burton, based on the stage musical of the same name. The film follows Benjamin Barker, a barber who returns to London after years of wrongful imprisonment, now calling himself Sweeney Todd. Consumed by rage and a thirst for revenge against the corrupt Judge Turpin who destroyed his life, Todd sets up a barbershop above Mrs. Lovett’s struggling meat pie shop. Together, they form a macabre partnership: Todd murders unsuspecting customers, and Mrs. Lovett disposes of the bodies by baking them into her pies. As Todd’s obsession with vengeance grows, his humanity fades, and he becomes a relentless figure of death. The gothic atmosphere, with shadowy streets and haunting visuals, perfectly complements the film’s grim tone. Johnny Depp delivers a chilling yet charismatic performance as Todd, while Helena Bonham Carter brings a twisted charm to Mrs. Lovett. The film’s music, composed by Stephen Sondheim, mixes eerie beauty with moments of brutal intensity. Tragic revelations build toward a shocking and bloody climax, where vengeance comes at a devastating cost. Sweeney Todd is ultimately a story of love lost, justice twisted, and revenge that consumes everything in its path.
