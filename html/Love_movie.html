SPOTSTAR - LOVE

Love is a Malayalam psychological thriller directed by Khalid Rahman that unfolds entirely within the confines of an apartment. The story centers on a troubled couple, Deepthi and Anoop, whose toxic relationship forms the core of the narrative. As the film progresses, what begins as a domestic dispute quickly spirals into a dark and unsettling exploration of the human psyche. The movie plays with perception, blurring the lines between reality and imagination, leaving the audience constantly guessing. The confined setting intensifies the claustrophobic tension, and the minimal cast allows for a deep dive into the emotional instability of the characters. The narrative structure is unconventional, jumping between timelines and perspectives, creating an eerie and suspenseful atmosphere. The film questions the nature of guilt, control, and repression within relationships. It also explores themes like mental health and emotional abuse in a chilling, layered manner. The cinematography and sound design amplify the sense of unease, while the tight screenplay keeps viewers on edge. As the mystery unravels, the audience is forced to confront uncomfortable truths about love, possession, and self-destruction. The movie ends with an open-ended ambiguity that lingers long after the credits roll.
