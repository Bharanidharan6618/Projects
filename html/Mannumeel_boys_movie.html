SPOTSTAR - MANJUMMEL BOYS

Manjummel Boys is a gripping Malayalam survival thriller inspired by true events. The story follows a group of friends from the small town of Manjummel who set out on a fun trip to Kodaikanal. Their carefree adventure takes a dark turn when one of them accidentally falls into the treacherous Guna Caves, a place infamous for its dangerous terrain and narrow passages. What starts as a joyful journey quickly becomes a desperate rescue mission filled with tension, fear, and unwavering friendship. The film beautifully portrays the bond between the friends, showing how loyalty and courage can overcome even the most terrifying obstacles. Each character is given depth, making the audience feel the emotional weight of their struggles. The cinematography captures both the beauty of nature and the claustrophobic dread of the caves. The background score heightens the suspense, keeping viewers on the edge of their seats. As the friends risk their own lives to save one of their own, the movie becomes a powerful tribute to unity, sacrifice, and the unbreakable spirit of friendship. It’s a heart-pounding yet emotional ride that lingers in the mind long after it ends.
