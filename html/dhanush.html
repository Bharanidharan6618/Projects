SPOTSTAR - DHANUSH

DHANUSH

Dhanush is a highly acclaimed Indian actor, director, producer, playback singer, and lyricist who primarily works in Tamil cinema. Born on July 28, 1983, in Chennai as Venkatesh Prabhu Kasthuri Raja, he is the son of filmmaker Kasthuri Raja and brother of director Selvaraghavan. Despite initial criticism for his unconventional looks, Dhanush quickly rose to stardom with his raw talent and versatile performances. He gained widespread recognition with films like Kadhal Kondein, Pudhupettai, Polladhavan, and Aadukalam, the latter earning him a National Film Award for Best Actor. His portrayal of emotionally complex characters has made him one of the most respected actors in Indian cinema. Dhanush also earned pan-Indian fame with the viral song "Why This Kolaveri Di," which became a cultural phenomenon. His Bollywood debut in Raanjhanaa showcased his acting prowess to a wider audience and brought him critical acclaim. He later appeared in international cinema through the English-language film The Extraordinary Journey of the Fakir and the Hollywood action-thriller The Gray Man. Apart from acting, he is a successful producer under his banner Wunderbar Films and has introduced fresh talents to the industry. Known for his passion and dedication, Dhanush constantly experiments with genres, from action to drama and even fantasy. His recent roles in films like Asuran, Karnan, and Captain Miller have further solidified his place as a powerhouse performer. Despite his success, he remains humble and grounded, admired by fans for both his simplicity and skill. Dhanush continues to be a driving force in Indian cinema, constantly redefining the boundaries of performance and storytelling.
