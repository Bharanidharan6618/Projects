SPOTSTAR - NARIVATTA

Narivetta is a Malayalam political action drama directed by Anuraj Manohar. The film follows the journey of Varghese Peter, a newly recruited police constable who is reluctantly posted to a tribal region in Wayanad. Initially indifferent, Varghese slowly becomes aware of the oppression faced by the tribal communities. His transformation begins under the mentorship of Head Constable Basheer Ahammed, a compassionate officer who treats the locals with respect and empathy. However, when Basheer dies under suspicious circumstances during a state-led operation, Varghese is forced to confront the system he serves. The narrative unfolds through a non-linear structure, revealing hidden layers of political manipulation and injustice. Tovino Thomas delivers a strong performance as Varghese, portraying his inner turmoil with subtle intensity. Suraj Venjaramoodu brings warmth and depth to Basheer’s character. The film is grounded in real-life events and reflects on the power dynamics between the state and marginalized communities. Its visuals capture both the beauty and the tension of the forested landscape. With gripping storytelling and emotional weight, Narivetta stands out as a socially conscious film that challenges viewers to reflect on truth, duty, and resistance.
