SPOTSTAR - KATHTHI

Kaththi is a gripping Tamil action drama that tackles social issues through a powerful and engaging storyline. The film follows Kathiresan, a small-time criminal who escapes from prison and accidentally assumes the identity of Jeeva, a social activist who looks just like him. As he steps into Jeeva’s world, Kathiresan learns about the plight of farmers and villagers who are being exploited by a corrupt corporate giant. Moved by their suffering, he decides to fight for their rights, using his wit and courage to challenge the system. Vijay delivers a compelling dual performance, balancing the transformation of a selfish man into a reluctant hero. Samantha plays the female lead, adding charm and emotional depth to the story. A. R. Murugadoss directs the film with a sharp focus on both action and social relevance. The screenplay blends mass appeal with a strong message about land rights and corporate exploitation. Anirudh Ravichander’s music enhances the film’s emotional and dramatic moments. The dialogues are sharp, the action sequences are intense, and the theme is timely. Kaththi stands out as a commercial entertainer with a conscience, highlighting the power of one man’s voice against injustice.
