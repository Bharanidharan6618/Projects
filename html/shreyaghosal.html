SPOTSTAR - SHREYA GHOSHAL

SHREYA GHOSHAL

Shreya Ghoshal is one of India’s most celebrated and versatile playback singers, known for her melodious voice, flawless technique, and emotional expression. Born on March 12, 1984, in Berhampore, West Bengal, she began singing at a very young age and received classical music training from her mother. She gained national fame after winning the television reality show Sa Re Ga Ma at the age of sixteen. Her big break came when filmmaker Sanjay Leela Bhansali selected her to sing for his film Devdas, which earned her a National Award and several accolades. Since then, Shreya has sung in multiple Indian languages including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Bengali, and Marathi, becoming a household name across the country. Her voice has graced countless hit songs in films like Jodhaa Akbar, Bajirao Mastani, Padmaavat, and many more. She is known for her versatility, effortlessly handling classical, romantic, folk, and contemporary styles. Over the years, she has won numerous Filmfare Awards, National Awards, and state honors for her outstanding contribution to music. Shreya is also recognized globally, having performed in prestigious venues around the world. She is often praised not only for her musical talent but also for her humility and grace. In addition to playback singing, she has also worked as a judge on various music reality shows. Her voice continues to inspire aspiring singers and touches millions of hearts. Shreya Ghoshal remains a shining jewel in the Indian music industry, admired for both her artistry and character.
