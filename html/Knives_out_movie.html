SPOTSTAR - KNIVES OUT

Knives Out is a modern whodunit mystery that revolves around the sudden death of wealthy crime novelist Harlan Thrombey. The story unfolds in his sprawling estate, where eccentric family members gather for his birthday, only to be drawn into a tense investigation after his apparent suicide. Private detective Benoit Blanc is mysteriously hired to look into the case, suspecting foul play. As Blanc questions each family member, their greed, secrets, and lies begin to surface, revealing a web of motives. The central focus shifts to Marta Cabrera, Harlan’s kind-hearted nurse, who holds a crucial secret about the events of that night. The film cleverly twists the usual murder mystery formula, blending humor, suspense, and sharp social commentary. Every character is colorful yet suspicious, making the audience constantly guess the truth. The pacing keeps viewers engaged as layers of deception are peeled away. By the end, the mystery unravels in a surprising yet satisfying manner, proving that nothing is quite as it first appears. The film stands out for its wit, unique narrative structure, and unexpected resolution.
